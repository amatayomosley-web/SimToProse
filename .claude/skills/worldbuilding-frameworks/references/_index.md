# worldbuilding-frameworks — reference index

The deep reference files for the `worldbuilding-frameworks` skill. **These are authored in a later pass**; this index is the plan. The parent `SKILL.md` holds the one-line framework catalogue and the how-to-use method — read it first. Each file below expands its `SKILL.md` section: for each framework, the deep treatment adds *what it is*, *the real-world provenance / key source*, *the Limit or contested edge*, *a vivid exemplar*, and *how to instantiate it in a world bible* (schema → archetype → perturb). Every file stays **framework-neutral** (rival schools held side by side) and **grounds the invented in the real**.

Files are ordered to the world workflow (`docs/world-model.md`: law → community → planet → history → present systems), with the cross-cutting method file last.

---

### 1. `physical-geography.md` — the stage's bones
**Feeds:** planet (step 2) — geology → resource spread → climate → biomes.
**Covers:** plate tectonics & the Wilson cycle; orogeny, hotspots, isostasy/erosion; atmospheric circulation (Hadley/Ferrel/Polar cells), Coriolis & prevailing winds, orographic lift & rain shadow; ocean currents & thermohaline circulation; Köppen–Geiger (+ Trewartha), Whittaker, and Holdridge climate/biome classifications; Milankovitch cycles; the biome catalogue; drainage basins & the hydrological cycle, Strahler order & Horton's laws; fluvial/karst/glacial/aeolian landforms; coastlines & sea level; **ore genesis, fossil-fuel formation, and resource spread** as the conflict substrate; pedology; biogeographic realms & Wallace's Line.
**Spine:** the causal chain tectonics → terrain → climate → soil → where life and wealth sit — so geography is *derived*, not decorated. Foregrounds **uneven resource distribution** as the load-bearing output (planet.md's first-class field). Includes a "build a plausible map in the right order" walkthrough and a technical-worldbuilding pointer set (climate-cookbook resources).

### 2. `ecology-and-evolution.md` — what lives here, and why
**Feeds:** planet (step 2) — ecology & creature habitats.
**Covers:** natural & sexual selection; niche & competitive exclusion; trophic levels & the 10% rule; food webs, keystone species, trophic cascades, ecosystem engineers; r/K selection; carrying capacity & logistic growth; Lotka–Volterra dynamics; island biogeography; adaptive radiation, convergent evolution, coevolution & the Red Queen; speciation, founder effect & drift; punctuated equilibrium; mass extinction & recovery; latitudinal diversity gradient; Bergmann's & Allen's rules; biomimicry, functional morphology & the square-cube law; the speculative-biology (Dixon) tradition.
**Spine:** designing life and creatures that are **evolutionarily earned** — a monster from its niche, a food web from its energy budget — so ecology grounds both the mundane dangerous beast (planet) and, where law permits, the supernatural one's *presence*. Carries the discipline that a creature's Limit falls out of its biology.

### 3. `economic-systems.md` — the material base
**Feeds:** present systems (step 4) — economy; planet — resource stakes.
**Covers:** modes of subsistence; surplus & the origin of complexity (Childe); substantivism vs formalism; Polanyi's forms of integration (reciprocity/redistribution/exchange); Sahlins' reciprocity spectrum; gift economy (Mauss), Kula & potlatch; prestige-goods & sumptuary economies; the emergence & theory of money (metallism vs chartalism); debt as social relation (Graeber); scarcity/supply-and-demand; comparative advantage & division of labor; trade networks & routes; central place theory; the Malthus–Boserup pair; property & surplus capture; labor organization; the economic archetypes; the three dramatic drivers (what's scarce / who holds the surplus / who depends on whom).
**Spine:** economy as the agnostic schema of `present-systems.md` — scarcity inherited from the planet, surplus setting social complexity — modeled to *circumstance-depth*, not textbook simulation. Threads the **specials hook** (how magic/a unique resource/undead labor warp the schema).

### 4. `political-and-power-systems.md` — order and its structure
**Feeds:** present systems (step 4) — governance & factions; history — state formation.
**Covers:** Service's and Fried's sociopolitical typologies; Weber's three authorities & the monopoly-on-violence; Aristotle's six regimes & Polybius' anacyclosis; Ibn Khaldun's asabiyyah; Montesquieu's separation of powers; Mann's IEMP; Lukes' three faces of power; Carneiro's circumscription; Tilly's bellicism; Wittfogel's hydraulic hypothesis; segmentary lineage & acephalous order; patron–client & principal–agent structures; divine kingship; the governance/legal archetypes; faction-as-collective-character.
**Spine:** how legitimacy, force, and administration compose into a governable (or ungovernable) order — and how power's fault lines become **factions**, the director's primary lever-source. Ties governance to the legal preset's `Source/Legislator/Enforcer` dimensions and to history's live tensions.

### 5. `religion-and-mythology.md` — the sacred, structured
**Feeds:** universal law (step 1) — metaphysics; present systems (step 4) — religion-as-lived; history — belief as a driver.
**Covers:** the **real-vs-believed (Epistemic) axis** as the master fork; types of religious system; pantheon design & theogony; Dumézil's trifunctional hypothesis; Campbell's monomyth (*with its critics*) & his four functions of myth; Lévi-Strauss's structuralism; Durkheim's sacred/profane; Weber's routinization of charisma; Eliade's sacred space & eternal return; Otto's numinous; Geertz's religion-as-cultural-system; myth typologies; van Gennep's rites of passage & Turner's liminality/communitas; Propp's morphology & the Aarne–Thompson–Uther index; Frazer's magic→religion→science; theodicy; syncretism, millenarianism & cargo cults.
**Spine:** two jobs at once — the **structure** of a believable faith (pantheon, myth, ritual) *and* its **truth-status** (law's Epistemic check: real gods vs sincere false belief), which decides how every believer is simulated. Keeps Campbell held with his over-application critique, per the framework-neutral rule.

### 6. `magic-and-the-supernatural.md` — designing the impossible with teeth
**Feeds:** universal law (step 1) — the magic/beings switches; present systems (step 4) — magic's role.
**Covers:** Sanderson's First/Second/Third/Zeroth Laws; the hard–soft spectrum; cost/limitation/consequence; Frazer's sympathetic magic (similarity & contagion); mana (the anthropological concept); true-name magic; Vancian/preparatory magic; elemental & correspondence systems; the source/wielder/cost/limit design schema; the supernatural-beings switch (each with its existence–Limit–Epistemic triple); magic's Epistemic status; and the **specials hook** for magic's economic/political role.
**Spine:** the project's non-negotiable — every "yes, magic exists" ships with its **Limit (teeth)** and **Epistemic status**, so the world can still refuse a lever and the probe can fail honestly. Grounds invented magic in real folk-logic (sympathetic magic, mana, true names) rather than free invention.

### 7. `social-structure-and-kinship.md` — who counts as us
**Feeds:** present systems (step 4) — culture/custom; the space of character positions (Phase B).
**Covers:** descent systems; lineage/clan/moiety/phratry; the six kinship terminology systems (Morgan); marriage & residence rules; alliance vs descent theory; stratification (caste/estate/class/slavery, ascribed vs achieved); age sets & grades; Dunbar's number; honor/dignity/face cultures; guilt vs shame (Benedict); Hofstede's dimensions; Douglas's grid/group; cultural materialism (Harris); in-group/out-group & ethnocentrism; culture-as-collective-model (a shared weighting over the worth-menu).
**Spine:** the custom preset of `present-systems.md` — a culture as a shared weighting over values + a kinship/status structure — and the **multi-culture contact layer** where in-group/out-group boundaries become friction, factions, and the formative baselines characters are generated from.

### 8. `language-and-naming.md` — the sound of a world
**Feeds:** present systems (step 4) — culture; the naming surface across all layers.
**Covers:** phoneme inventory & phonotactics; morphological typology; language families & the comparative method; regular sound change (Grimm's Law); writing systems; toponymy & anthroponymy; onomastic layering (substrate names); conlang depth levels (naming-language → full grammar); sound symbolism/phonaesthetics; Zipf's law; linguistic relativity (Sapir–Whorf, weak form); Tolkien's language-first method.
**Spine:** matching **linguistic effort to what the story levers** — usually a *naming language* (consistent phonology, no grammar) is enough — while giving names the internal consistency that makes a culture feel real. Toponymy as fossilized history that writes the map; naming conventions as badges of lineage and status.

### 9. `history-and-societal-change.md` — the rise and fall engine
**Feeds:** history (step 3) — causation; present systems — live tensions.
**Covers:** Ibn Khaldun's asabiyyah cycle; Turchin's structural-demographic theory, secular cycles & elite overproduction; Tainter's diminishing-returns collapse; Diamond's geographic determinism (*Guns, Germs, and Steel*) & the *Collapse* five-driver framework; Toynbee's challenge-and-response; Spengler's organic cycles; Quigley's instrument→institution; Braudel's longue durée; the great-man/structural/contingent debate; McNeill's disease-in-history; Crosby's Columbian Exchange; path dependence & lock-in; diffusion vs independent invention; demographic transition; environmental/climate causation (Bronze Age collapse, 4.2-kiloyear event, Little Ice Age).
**Spine:** the mechanisms that let history be **authored backward and validated forward** — causal engines (not chronologies) for war, collapse, and stagnation that make a demanded present *earned by conditions*, and that end loaded with the live tensions the director levers. Holds determinism and contingency as rival poles, per premise.

### 10. `technology-and-material-culture.md` — the level of the possible
**Feeds:** cross-cutting — planet → history → present systems.
**Covers:** the three-age system; Lenski's sociocultural evolution; White's energy-capture law; Childe's revolutions; the tech-tree & prerequisite chains; Lynn White's device-driven social change; Rogers's diffusion of innovations; metallurgy stages; key hinge-inventions (plow, wheel, sail, writing, printing, gunpowder, mill); appropriate & path-dependent technology; the Kardashev scale; and the coupling of tech-level to economy, war, governance, and daily life.
**Spine:** technology as the **bound on the possible** at every layer — what can be grown, traded, built, and fought with — treated as a dependency graph so a world's level stays internally consistent (no steel without its whole chain), and as a social hinge (a single device reordering a society), while resisting a linear "advancement" ladder.

### 11. `worldbuilding-method-and-grounding.md` — how to build with the well
**Feeds:** all layers — the discipline itself.
**Covers:** Tolkien's sub-creation & secondary belief; the iceberg/1% theory; the real-world analog method **and its reskinning pitfall**; architect vs gardener; the MICE quotient; authored-backward/validated-forward; the depth rule (hinges, not census); "worldbuilding disease"; the no-contradiction floor; and the Limit + Epistemic checks as working habits.
**Spine:** the meta-file that ties every framework above back to the project's own disciplines — ground the invented in the real, author only what's levered, give every "yes" teeth and a truth-status, and keep the world able to refuse a lever. Where the well's philosophy (a neutral, forkable draw-from, never a prescribed method) is stated in full.

## Authoring notes for the later pass
- **One file per section of `SKILL.md`, same order, same names** — the index and the catalogue stay in lockstep.
- **Per framework, five fields:** what it is · real-world provenance + key source (accurate, cited, never invented) · its Limit / contested edge · a vivid exemplar · how to instantiate (schema → archetype → perturb).
- **Neutrality is mandatory:** where schools rival (determinism/contingency, alliance/descent, hard/soft magic), present both and name the tension; the premise chooses, not the file.
- **Ground everything:** no invented dimension without its earthly analog; verify names, dates, and attributions before writing.
- **Cross-link to `docs/`:** each file points back to the world-workflow step it feeds (`universal-law.md`, `broader-community.md`, `planet.md`, `history.md`, `present-systems.md`, `world-state-ledger.md`) and to the shared worth-menu (`values-and-stakes.md`).
