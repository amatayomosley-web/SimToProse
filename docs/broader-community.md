# Broader Community — the scope gate (before the planet)

> Sits **between universal law and planet** in the world workflow. THE gate: does the story's world stand alone, or sit inside a **wider community of worlds/peoples** (interplanetary → interstellar → intergalactic)? **No → say no, design the planet standalone. Yes → define it (to the depth the planet needs) *before* the planet**, because the community *constrains* the planet. Rubric, not content — same as `universal-law.md`.

## Why it comes before the planet
A world embedded in a wider community is **not designed in isolation** — the community is **upstream**, constraining the planet downstream:
- its **resources** are things others want or supply (trade, extraction, scarcity set off-world);
- its **ecology** may carry introduced/engineered species or contamination;
- its **history** may include contact, colonization, war, or abandonment;
- its **present status** may be membership, subjugation, protectorate, or quarantine.

So the community's **existence** and **this world's place in it** must be settled first; the planet inherits the frame. (A capital world and a forgotten backwater are different *planets*, not just different politics.)

**Law enables; this step instantiates.** Whether a wider community is even *possible* — many worlds + travel/contact between them — is settled in `universal-law.md` domain E (extent & reachability). This step asks, given it's possible, whether one **actually exists** and what it is.

## The gate
**Is there a known broader community?**
- **No** → record "standalone world," skip the rest, go to the planet.
- **Yes** → define the rubric below, to the **depth the planet levers on** (the hinges touching *this* world; lazy-resolve the rest of the galaxy — don't build a galaxy to tell a story on one world).

## If yes — what must be defined (the rubric)
1. **Membership (who):** one people across many worlds (a diaspora) or **many peoples / species**? (Non-human sapients are a `universal-law.md` B/biology *existence* switch — settle existence there, identity here.)
2. **Connection (how):** what binds them — **travel** (FTL / gates / slow ships), **communication** (instant / lightspeed-lagged / none), trade, shared institutions? **+ Limit (teeth):** what *can't* cross or connect — the lag, the cost, the closed border. Without a limit the community has no constraints and the probe goes hollow.
3. **Structure (the order):** a polity (empire / federation / hegemony), a loose contact-network, a market, a cold standoff, or the **ruins of a fallen order**? The political shape.
4. **This world's place in it** — *the load-bearing field for planet design:* core / colony / frontier / protectorate / contacted-but-independent / quarantined / abandoned / **unaware-it's-watched**. This is what the planet inherits; everything else is context for it.
5. **Contact status & history:** first contact done or pending? at peace / trade / war / cold-war? what has *this* world's relationship been — visited, colonized, raided, ignored? (Feeds the planet's history, the next-but-one step.)
6. **Epistemic (who knows):** do *this* world's inhabitants **know** the community exists — fully integrated, watched-without-knowing, or is it myth / secret / heresy? (Ties to `knowledge-model.md`: common-knowledge vs secret vs false belief — the same known/believed check as the law guide.)

## Cross-cutting checks (same discipline as law)
- **The Limit (teeth).** For every reach the community has, state what it **cannot** do *here* — the empire's writ doesn't run to the frontier; comms lag means no real-time control; the treaty forbids landing. The teeth that let this world's situation plausibly **refuse** a community-lever (or the probe can't fail honestly).
- **The Epistemic status.** Known / believed / secret, per element, to this world's people. The community can be *real but hidden*, or *believed but absent* — and the inhabitants are simulated on their belief, not the truth.

## Meta-rules (inherited)
- **Default is NO** — standalone world unless the premise needs the wider community. (Sparse authoring at the cosmic scale.)
- **Serve the premise** — the community exists to serve the conceit, not for grandeur.
- **Depth rule** — define only what the planet and story lever on; the rest is "undetermined — fill when levered."
- **Downstream inherits** — the planet, its history, and its present must be consistent with this; if planet design needs to violate it, revise here.

## Output
Either **"standalone world"** (→ planet), or a community defined to planet-depth: membership · connection (+Limit) · structure · **this world's place** · contact status · who-knows. The planet is then designed *within* that frame.
