## The naming convention, and why it is load-bearing

**A path takes the name of its FLOOR, never its peak.** The floor is the entry; the peak is where it
ends. Naming by the peak invites authoring toward the peak, which is exactly how RAGE came to hold
five clusters of trait words and a ladder that forked three times.

Two paths break the convention deliberately and the tension is surfaced, not silently resolved:
**SELF-REGARD** would be PRIDE by conversation habit (`pride` is rung 3), and **LEVITY** would be
AMUSEMENT by the convention (`amusement` is rung 1). Both keep the established name.

## The consumption meta-form

Six of the nine read *"how much of you X has taken."* That is not a coincidence and not a template
applied for tidiness — it was arrived at twice by correction. GOODWILL was first built as a RATIO
(how much of the reckoning is theirs vs yours) and SELF-REGARD as a MAGNITUDE; both were wrong, and
both became consumption axes. A consumption axis needs no denominator, which retired a whole
"unstored denominator" gap that was an artifact of the ratio framing.

## 2. DISPLEASURE — anger is control coming off, in order

**BUILT AND MEASURED. `docs/rungs/DISPLEASURE.md` is the authoritative artifact; this section is a
pointer.** Blocks authored, blind-sorted clean at **mean rho 0.935**, resolved by `rungs.rung_at`,
selected by `scripts/composer.py`. The first path to leave design.

    floor  displeasure — a standard has not been met, and nothing follows
    peak   amok

**THE AXIS CHANGED, and the old wording here was the reason the ladder kept forking.** "How much of
your response is still under your own supervision" was right and too abstract to author against. The
usable form: **a rung names WHICH control has gone**, and the controls fail outside-in —
`concealment → attention → delivery → stopping → restraint → selection → termination → presence`.
A control does not snap; it costs first, then goes, which is what yields twelve rungs from eight
controls.

**THE FAILED SORT RECORDED HERE IS SUPERSEDED.** The rho 0.895 / five-of-twelve / fork-in-every-run
result was against a list containing `grievance · resentment · rancor · hatred · grudge · implacable` —
standing dispositions toward one person, on the ARC clock, sitting beside per-beat states. Two clocks
are two questions and no prose reconciles them. Evicted to the `toward` tier; the fork went with them.

**THE 10/11 ORDER WAS INVERTED AND IS FIXED — on an argument, not on corroboration.** Three rewriters
found it, but traced 2026-09-07 they are not three origins: two were one model sampled twice, and all
three read the same brief, which itself contained the spread column showing the discrepancy. The
reasoning is what stands, and it needs no witness: tribal targeting — their people, their things — needs MORE discrimination than
hitting whoever is nearest, so it cannot sit above it on a control axis. Now fury → rage → berserk →
amok, recovered with zero error by every sorter since.

**BANDS (calibration, not derived — `scripts/gen_rungs.py`).** Width is how much runway a state needs
before it should promote: lived-in states absorb pressure without reclassifying, rare states escalate
on a small increment. Widest at chafing and bristling (.13 each) where the cast actually rests; the
top three own .09 together.

**HOLD, retained from the earlier measurement:** provocation size and the loud register. A large
provocation spends the quiet act-kinds by rung 3, and muting every beat deletes the loud register
wholesale. **Fails if** sorters rank by volume or by which object absorbs the force.

⚠ **KNOWN DEVIATION AT RUNG 6.** Seven sorters, every one placing *seething* two rungs too high; two
rewrites failed. Sorters rank by felt intensity and the axis is control, and rung 6 is the single
place those disagree — maximum feeling, maximum command. Characterised, not repaired.

⚠ **NAME COLLISION, unresolved.** `emotion-scales.md:126-131` reserves annoyance / anger / fury /
rage as REGION names; this ladder uses all four as RUNG names at different heights.

The ceiling is `sacrifice` on the engine's own warrant: `emotion-list.md:98` is the five-anchor row

⚠ `sacrifice` names an ACT, which the rung rules disfavour. Kept: single, taggable, in the corpus,

**THE FINDING WORTH KEEPING:** English names total consumption by another's welfare only FROM THE

coin only if tagging demands it.

**NOT RUNGS:** doting / oversolicitousness / overprotectiveness are a CALIBRATION FLAG

dial reversed. pity / paternalism are RANK, on the existing respect edge. savior-complex /
martyr-complex / codependency are a GOODWILL height plus existing edge fields, not a new path.

⚠ **ENGINE BLOCKER, live:** `emotion-scales.md:184` — CARE is *"raised by care_relevant +0.40 ·
lowered by NOTHING."* Care can never fall, so no character descends out of enmeshment.

## 6. SELF-REGARD — how much of you your own standing has taken

The exact mirror of GOODWILL. One consumes you outward and ends at `sacrifice`; this consumes you
inward and ends at `apotheosis`. Same shape, opposite door.

    zero   self-forgetfulness / egolessness / unselfconsciousness
           [the absorbed craftsman is not estimating himself modestly — he is not estimating
           himself at all]
     1  self-regard       you count, to yourself. At rest, costs nothing
     2  dignity           a standing you would notice being touched
     3  pride             your standing is attached to something you did
     4  self-importance   you weigh more than the business in front of you
     5  vanity            your presentation must be maintained
     6  conceit           the estimate has stopped checking itself
     7  arrogance         others exist as audience or obstacle
     8  hubris            you are exempt from what binds everyone
     9  narcissism      ~ others have no interior; they are supply, not people
    10  megalomania       the estimate has replaced the world
    11  apotheosis        the estimate has replaced YOU

The top five are one sequence of WHAT STOPS BEING REAL: others are below you → the rules do not
apply to you → others have no insides → the world conforms to you → you are no longer a person. Each
rung removes one more thing's independent reality. Arrogance still grants others FORCE; an obstacle
has force. Narcissism does not.

`hubris` ABSORBS `grandiosity`: grandiosity is SIZE (no measure contains you), hubris is EXEMPTION
(the rules do not bind you) — different KINDS, not amounts. Hubris keeps the slot because it
PRODUCES ACTION; grandiosity can sit inert in a fantasist.

`diffidence` was CUT, and that removal is what took the valence fork off this ladder — the fork
structurally identical to RAGE's hot/cold split, which failed three blind sorts. Self-doubt and
diffidence belong to an EFFICACY quantity (self-doubt, irresolution, vacillation, assurance,
self-confidence, self-belief, self-trust, self-efficacy), which is separated out and unbuilt.

⚠ `narcissism` and `megalomania` carry a CLINICAL-USAGE flag: modern usage is diagnostic, so a prose
reader may emit them as a DIAGNOSIS rather than a state read. Applied to both or neither.
⚠ **Predicted collapse:** self-importance / vanity DISSOCIATE — the chairman who thinks his views
matter but not how he looks is self-important and not vain. Words that dissociate are not reliably
one above the other; this is the pair a blind sorter is expected to collapse or invert.

## 7. DISTASTE — how far the expulsion must reach

From your mouth, to your body, to the room, to the world, to everyone who touched it. Built on the
ACTION TENDENCY, which is why it is SHORT: disgust has fewer distinct behaviours than rage or care.

    zero   clean / at home in the world / unsqueamishness

    #  rung             vector      the mentality                what they DO
    1  distaste       0.00-0.15   this is not to be taken in     declines it; nobody notices
    2  squeamishness  0.15-0.35   I won't have it on me          distance, won't touch
    3  revulsion      0.35-0.60   it must be gone from here      body recoils, expels
    4  loathing       0.60-0.82   its EXISTING is the offence    stops avoiding; acts to remove it
    5  abomination    0.82-1.00   it contaminates by contact     drives it out, shuns what touched it

**THOUGHT/ACTION LINE AT 0.15 — the lowest of any path.** Every other path spends most of its range
interior; this one goes physical almost immediately, consistent with a body-level primitive rather
than a judgment.

**RUNG 5 IS WHY THE PATH EXISTS.** `abomination` is the mentality that SPREADS — anyone near the
unclean thing becomes unclean. It is what produces exile, shunning, a crowd turning on someone. No
other path generates that behaviour.

**WHAT ONLY THIS EXPRESSES:** CONTAMINATION — something will get into or onto you and you will not
get it off. Not "I don't want it" (stirring at zero), not "it might harm me" (wariness: harm is
external and future; contamination is already on you), not "it broke a standard" (displeasure). And
the property that makes it primitive rather than a flavour of aversion: **it does not answer to
reason.** You can know the plate is clean and still not eat off it.

**CONTEMPT COMES OFF THIS PATH.** ~16 outward words are RANK, not contamination (condescension,
superiority, superciliousness, disdain, snobbery, contempt, sneering, scorn, derision, despisal).
The decisive test is behavioural: **rank produces no expulsion.** A contemptuous person STAYS, and
looks down. Goes to the respect edge, as rank did for GOODWILL.

**SELF-DISGUST IS A SEPARATE PATH, UNBUILT** — 160 names ready. Pointed at the self, expulsion
becomes WITHDRAWAL, which is a different action, so the target test splits it out (below). Its
corpus measures 26 contamination · 18 social exposure · 14 moral self-judgment; only the first is
contamination-shaped, and exposure-shame may belong elsewhere.

## 8. DEFLATION — how much of you the lowness has taken

    floor  deflation — the air going out of something that had it
    peak   despair

Ten rungs: deflation · dejection · sadness · sorrow · misery · inconsolability · despondency ·
despair · devastation · hollowness. Two independent framings placed `despondency` and `despair` at
near-identical heights from a seed containing neither, and both put the ceiling boundary at exactly
0.90.

**THE HINGE at ~0.60. BELOW it the currency is SCOPE; ABOVE it, FACULTIES.** Below, how much of
experience is coloured; above, which faculty is gone.
**Fails if sorters rank by HURT.** Above the hinge, hurt and capacity come APART, because part of
what is removed is the capacity to feel the loss sharply — despondency reads as LESS painful than
sorrow while being further along. Every block above the hinge must be written against what is GONE,
never against how much it stings. **Scope: DEFLATION, above the hinge only** — one
path, one mechanism (hurt and capacity dissociate when capacity is what is being removed), reasoned
rather than measured. **It stops BELOW the hinge**, where the currency is scope and stinging is the
honest read; and it does not transfer to the other eight paths, none of which removes the faculty
that registers its own axis.

**NO BOND-LOSS PATH. NO IDENTITY-GRIEF PATH.** Both are DEFLATION with a target. Verified by walking
all ten rungs against a heartbroken person: every rung fits, and the hinge lands exactly where
heartbreak turns — the first weeks colour everything, then people stop being able to reach her.

8 of these 10 rungs sit in the PANIC_GRIEF pools. That pool is not a rival corpus; **it IS the
sadness pool, mis-filed under a peak-name.** DEFLATION inherits two tag fields from it: the `recall`
KIND (the one primitive the engine admits can be re-triggered by memory rather than by an event in
the room) and the slow decay — grief is supposed to outlast its cause.

⚠ RECORDED ERROR: `heartbreak = DEFLATION + STIRRING` was proposed and is WRONG. That is a COMPOUND,
which is the architecture the path model retires. The waves are not two paths interacting; they are
one state re-triggered by recall.

## 9. LEVITY — how much of your choosing you have handed to what is available

> **STATUS 2026-09-11: BUILT, by the owner's ruling** (*"Add levity back in, we will see if in production it
> fails, if so we repair"*). The nine blocks are in `docs/rungs/LEVITY.md`, written to this spine and
> unmeasured; the 2026-09-07 finding that the axis and rungs one to five disagree in performance stands
> there unrepaired, and production is the test. PLAY's appraisal pushes are restored under this name.

Not "how much fun" and not "how much of the accounting is suspended" — both discarded. The engine's
existing anchors map onto this axis exactly: 0.00 everything is instrumental · 0.25 you let a joke
through and do not chase it · 0.55 you would keep going past usefulness · 0.80 the purpose has
fallen away and you have not noticed · 1.00 there is no outside to this.

    zero   telic state / all business / everything instrumental
     1  amusement    0.00-0.10   an opening registers and pleases you; nothing handed over  thought
     2  levity       0.10-0.22   you let one through, and go back to it
     3  playfulness  0.22-0.36   you are disposed toward openings; you would take the next
     4  banter       0.36-0.50   the exchange is inside the frame; you say what you would not
     5  mischief     0.50-0.62   you HUNT openings, and push to see the frame hold
     6  immersion    0.62-0.74   you stop checking outside
     7  absorption   0.74-0.85   the activity sets the pace, not you
     8  raptness     0.85-0.94   the activity chooses; you follow
     9  flow         0.94-1.00   no gap between opening and act; no chooser

**THOUGHT/ACTION LINE at 0.10**, second lowest after DISTASTE — letting a joke through is already an
act, so only `amusement` is interior.

**IT IS THE ONLY PATH WITH NO OBJECT.** Goodwill needs a beneficiary, stirring a thing wanted,
wariness a threat, displeasure a wrong, distaste a contaminant. Levity is not ABOUT anything — it is
a mode the person is in, and the 1.00 anchor is one no other path could write.

**THE TAGGER'S TEST**, since a joke can be tagged GOODWILL just as easily: *is the joke FOR the
person, or for the joke?* "You're an idiot" to lift a friend who is low is GOODWILL — instrumental
to their welfare, and there is no reason to say it if you remove the friend. Said because the pun
was sitting there is LEVITY. Care at any height is instrumental to a welfare; play is the one thing
instrumental to nothing. **It is therefore the only path that argues for a NON-OPTIMAL action** —
without it every character is an optimiser, and optimisers are not people.

**THE MECHANISM IS RE-PRICING, NOT RELEASE.** Inside the frame an attempt is deniable — "I was only
joking" — so a character acts on their stirring, displeasure or goodwill at LOWER magnitude than
they otherwise would, because the act comes with a retreat. Courtship, challenge and testing loyalty
all run through it.

⚠ **NOT disinhibition**, and building it as such makes the actor write CARELESS characters.
Disinhibition removes the brake; levity changes what the brake MEASURES. A drunk says the cruel
thing because the check FAILED; a person in banter says it because the check PASSED — it ran, and
correctly found that inside this frame it does not land as cruelty. Three proofs: play is SELECTIVE
(you tease the one who can take it, about what they are secure in); play needs the frame SHARED, so
the player is continuously monitoring the other party, which is heavy processing rather than absent
processing; and **the engine settles it** — PLAY is lowered by threat −0.22, loss −0.20,
social_violation −0.15, where disinhibition would RISE under threat.
⚠ NOT a catalyst: a catalyst is not consumed, and consumption is this path's whole axis.
⚠ NOT Loki/trickster: a trickster is high-agency and wants the disruption; play at the top has LESS
agency, and rung 5 pushes to CONFIRM the frame, never to break it. Put "trickster" in the rung
language and the actor writes malice, which belongs near DISPLEASURE.

**THE FREE SCENE BEAT:** the frame is SHARED and can break ONE-SIDEDLY. One person steps out; the
other has not noticed. The joke that was affection three seconds ago lands as contempt — not because
it changed, but because the receiver left. The retreat closes behind the act: it was said, it was
meant, and now it counts. The engine produces this whenever two characters' levity diverges.

⚠ **OFF-AXIS, ~15 names deliberately NOT rungs:** giddiness, silliness, zaniness, goofiness,
high spirits, exuberance, ebullience, boisterousness, rambunctiousness, revelry. These measure
AROUSAL / EXPRESSIVENESS, not how much choosing has been handed over — a person can be quietly
absorbed (high on this axis) with zero silliness, and boisterous while still fully steering.
Admitting them would be the RAGE hot/cold fork again.
⚠ A FLAG, not a rung: `forced gaiety`, `hollow levity`, `strained jollity` — play PERFORMED without
the state. The sharpest thing in the corpus for character work: a person who HAS to seem light.

## THE TARGET TEST — what settles path-vs-slot

**Does the axis survive the target swap?** Same sentence with a different target → ONE path, and the
target is a slot. Sentence breaks or the quantity changes → TWO paths.

| path | the swap | verdict |
|---|---|---|
| DEFLATION | "how much of you the lowness has taken" — identical whether the target is a person, an inheritance, a career, or yourself | **ONE path, target is a slot** |
| GOODWILL | "how much of you your OWN welfare has taken" is incoherent — it does not mirror | **TWO paths** (+ the self-centred one) |
| DISTASTE | pointed at the self, expulsion becomes WITHDRAWAL — a different action | **TWO paths** (+ self-disgust) |

**THE CAUSE IS NEVER IN THE PATH.** The rung rules ban a cause inside a rung NAME; the target test
extends that to path MEMBERSHIP. `bereavement`, `heartbreak` and `homesickness` are not emotions —
they are DEFLATION plus a target type, the same way `jealousy` was rejected as a path.

This RETIRES an earlier proposed rule, "the self is always a separate path," which was over-read
from two cases. PANIC_GRIEF is `reflexive: yes` and `direction.py` calls it the most self-directed
primitive in the vocabulary — the third case went the other way, and the target test explains all
three without exception.

## The ladder files are POOLS, not paths

`docs/emotion-names/*.md` are NAME POOLS — words someone swept, to draw rungs from. They are not
rung lists, and their ORDER is not evidence of height. Measured: `SEEKING-reflexive.md` is three
brainstorm passes concatenated, so a word's position in it says nothing.

A COLLISION therefore only exists between two things THE ENGINE NAMES — never between a rung and a
pool word. Live collisions, checked: `affinity` (a RELATIONSHIP_AXES value), `salience` (the recall
gate's belief ranking), `attraction` (an appraisal dimension in `state._DIM_TO_PATH`).
`receptivity` is NOT a collision — it is only a pool word.
