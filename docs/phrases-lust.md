# LUST — the band sets

**Status: DRAFT, for review.** Forty bands at 0.05 steps. Rules as `docs/phrases-seeking.md`.

Regions (`docs/emotion-scales.md`): indifferent / attraction / desire / craving / consumed.

**LUST carries the RAGE trap.** The English word names the top region, so a low value reads as
lechery when it means *attraction*. The regions are the fix. `DIRECTEDNESS` refuses reflexive
(`records.py:91`), so the second table is UNBOUND.

**The channel LUST owns**, for the composition rule: **the bearing** — what the world is oriented
around, everything else measured as distance from union. SEEKING owns the next-question mechanism
and FEAR owns capacity, so this ceiling claims orientation and never attention-in-total.

## BOUND — someone is the draw

**The variable: how much of your attention the wanting has organised.**

**The competing axis this set must NOT encode:** **warmth.** Fondness, tenderness and concern for
their welfare are CARE — a band that renders as sweetness puts one note in two slots of the vector.

**Mood:** present indicative around a live possibility — the union stays prospective, the wanting is
now. The low end is habitual, because a resting value must not mint an attraction episode.

## UNBOUND — the wanting is real and the draw is not

**The variable: how much of your attention the wanting has organised, with no one it is aimed at.**

**The competing axis this set must NOT encode:** **the hunt.** Searching for someone to want is
SEEKING at a prospect. This is appetite without an errand — no band may send the actor scanning, or
it invents the object the numbers never aimed, which is the recorded defect `_UNBOUND_PHRASES` exists
to prevent.

## Notes

* **The thinnest primitive in the basis, measured**: one input dimension (`attraction`), dominant in
  none of the 41 compounds, capped at 0.25 in the two it appears in. Unreachable entirely until
  `attraction` was added 2026-08-22. Treat its calibration as the least settled of the eight.
* **Ceiling composition.** Bound claims only the bearing: with FEAR (the danger has the use of what
  is in you, the wanting among it), with CARE (selfless and oriented — consuming love), with SEEKING
  (the epistemic channel stays free — hunting a way to them), with PLAY (the flirtation beat: its own
  reason, and the world still all one direction). Unbound is the same channel in its broken form, and
  its hard pair is SEEKING — the seeker generates direction in the epistemic channel while the
  union-appetite has none. One person, one beat.
