# RAGE — the band set

**Status: DRAFT, for review.** Twenty bands at 0.05 steps. Rules as `docs/phrases-seeking.md`.

**The variable: how much of your response to a wrong is still under your own supervision.**

**The competing axis this set must NOT encode:** **volume.** MEASURED: at the top of this scale sound is subtracted, not added — 82% of officers in perceptual-distortion studies report diminished hearing. Shouting is the outside view. No band may encode loudness.

Regions (`docs/emotion-scales.md`): unmoved / annoyance / anger / fury / rage

| band | phrase |
|---|---|
| 0.00 | nothing here reads as a wrong to you |
| 0.05 | what lands does not stick |
| 0.10 | what registers is gone before it can matter |
| 0.15 | what lands sits somewhere out of the way, and you are not attending to it |
| 0.20 | nothing gets raised, and not everything gets forgotten |
| 0.25 | what registers, you let pass without marking |
| 0.30 | what goes unanswered has begun to collect in you |
| 0.35 | letting things pass has begun to cost you something |
| 0.40 | something in you has begun to want the slights acknowledged |
| 0.45 | letting things go has become a choice you can feel yourself making |
| 0.50 | not answering has become a decision you are making again and again |
| 0.55 | a sharpness is arriving before you choose it, and you notice it each time |
| 0.60 | you can hear yourself, and you are not correcting course |
| 0.65 | what it will cost you has stopped being the main question |
| 0.70 | you need the wrong named as wrong more than you need it mended |
| 0.75 | the place where you would ordinarily stop went by a moment ago |
| 0.80 | you are watching yourself do this, and you are not stopping |
| 0.85 | the part of you that would object is quieter than it was |
| 0.90 | the reasons to let it go are all still there and none of them weigh anything |
| 0.95 | you are not slowing, and nothing in you is asking you to |

## Notes

* **0.95 is a change in kind.** A 2026 *Aggressive Behavior* study found rage and anger episodes
  share triggers and physiology; what differs is controllability and awareness of one's own
  behaviour. The scale is supervision, and at the top there is no supervisor.
* **It is appetitive.** People deliberately make themselves angrier before a confrontation. Do not
  write the ceiling as an impairment nobody would choose.
