# The emotion vocabulary — exhaustive

**Status: DRAFT.** The showrunner's reference. Every nameable emotional state, with a definition,
the primitives it sits on, and its magnitude span.

**Who this is for.** The showrunner reads a scene blueprint and a character sheet, sees what just
happened, and writes the prompt for the actor. It names the emotion. The definitions here are what
let it pick the right word — the standard is a definition that would help someone choose between two
neighbours, not a gesture at a feeling.

**Provenance.** Ten domains swept independently (452 raw entries), then deduped, located against the
eight primitives, and merged. Magnitudes and placements are JUDGEMENT — no measurement calibrated
them. Entries marked ★ were added in the merge because the sweep missed them.

## CARE — OUTWARD

*Acting on another's behalf at cost to yourself. The sweep left a large hole here — the entire pity/compassion/mercy block was missing, and eight of the entries below are additions.*

### The bond cluster (ascending)

- **Liking** — mild positive regard for a person, finding their company or nature agreeable, with no felt stake in continued closeness. `CARE (outward) 0.05–0.25 + SEEKING (outward) low` · *vs* **fondness**: liking is evaluative with no bond yet; fondness would be missed if withdrawn.
- **Fondness** — settled, low-key affection built up through familiarity, carrying modest warmth and the beginning of a bond. `CARE (outward) 0.15–0.35 + PANIC_GRIEF (outward) very low — a thin thread that would register mild loss`
- **Warmth** — a felt glow of goodwill and ease toward someone, often triggered situationally by a kindness or a shared laugh rather than resting on an established bond. `CARE (outward) 0.20–0.40 + PLAY (outward) low` · *vs* **affection**: warmth is a passive glow; affection adds the urge to express it.
- **Bonhomie** — genial, easy good-fellowship: hearty friendliness of manner toward company in general, associated with sociable occasions. `CARE (outward, diffuse) 0.25–0.55`
- **Storge** — the easy, unchosen affection of long familiarity — family, or friends known since childhood — lower in drama than romantic love and unusually resilient. `CARE (outward) 0.30–0.55 + PANIC_GRIEF (outward), stable and low-arousal` · *vs* **fondness**: storge is grown by proximity and time rather than chosen.
- **Affection** — demonstrative tender feeling that seeks expression, through touch, words, or small acts, and toward increased closeness. `CARE (outward) 0.30–0.55 + SEEKING (outward)`
- **Friendship / philia** — mutual affection and goodwill between equals, grounded in shared activity, values, or history, necessarily reciprocal and without a sexual component. `CARE (outward) 0.30–0.60 + PLAY (outward) + PANIC_GRIEF (outward) low`
- **Sibling love** — the bond between siblings, combining affection and loyalty with an ordinary current of rivalry other kin bonds do not carry. `CARE (outward) 0.30–0.70 + PANIC_GRIEF (outward) + RAGE (outward) low`
- **Tenderness** — softened, protective gentleness evoked by another's fragility — a sleeping child, an injured friend — which quiets aggression rather than mobilising it. `CARE (outward, vulnerability-triggered) 0.35–0.60` · *vs* **protectiveness**: tenderness answers the vulnerability, protectiveness answers a threat to it.
- **Filial love / filial piety** — a child's or adult child's love, respect, and sense of obligation toward a parent, blending affection with duty. `CARE (outward) 0.40–0.75 + PANIC_GRIEF (outward)` ⚠H — the deference/duty component is a stance, not a drive.
- **Comradeship** — solidarity forged through shared hardship, risk, or effort — soldiers, teammates, crisis-coworkers — emphasising loyalty and mutual reliance over personal intimacy. `CARE (outward) 0.40–0.75 + PANIC_GRIEF (outward, sharpest under shared threat) + RAGE (outward, shared) + PLAY (outward)` · *vs* **friendship**: comradeship is forged by circumstance and often does not survive it ending.
- **Loyalty** — willed commitment to stand by a person or group at personal cost, largely independent of momentary affection: as much a decision as a feeling. `CARE (outward, with reflexive sacrifice of self-interest) 0.40–0.80` ⚠H · *vs* **trust**: trust is what you feel about their reliability; loyalty is what you do regardless of what you feel.
- **Companionate love** — the mellowed, deeply comfortable affection felt for a long-term partner: low arousal, high trust, built on habit and shared history rather than urgency. `CARE (outward) 0.50–0.75 + PANIC_GRIEF (outward, high but quiescent) + LUST (outward) low` · *vs* **passionate love**: the same bond in its other mode; they alternate across a relationship rather than replacing each other.
- **Protectiveness** — the urge to shield a loved one from harm, mobilising readiness to intervene or fight on their behalf. `CARE (outward) 0.50–0.85 + RAGE (outward, mobilised against the threat)`
- **Doting** — indulgent, excessive fondness that showers attention and forgives faults past reason, often read by onlookers as foolish. `CARE (outward) 0.55–0.80 + SEEKING (outward, compulsion to give)`
- **Adoration** — worshipful admiration fused with love, in which the beloved is experienced as wonderful and one is drawn to gaze, praise, and dwell on them. `SEEKING (outward) 0.70–0.90 + CARE (outward)` — listed here for cluster coherence; SEEKING carries the higher magnitude.
- **Devotion** — sustained, willed commitment to another's welfare at real cost, outlasting mood and not depending on the other currently being lovable. `CARE (outward) 0.60–0.85, subordinating one's own want + PANIC_GRIEF (outward)` ⚠H · *vs* **doting**: devotion can be effortful and joyless in the moment; doting asks nothing of the doter.
- **Paternal love** — a parent's bond toward offspring, culturally coded toward provision, guidance, and protection. `CARE (outward) 0.60–0.90 + RAGE (outward, protective) + SEEKING (outward, building for the child's future)`
- **Codependency / enmeshment** — a bond in which one's sense of self has become contingent on the other; caregiving is compulsive rather than chosen, and separation feels like self-annihilation. `CARE (outward, compulsive) 0.60–0.90 + PANIC_GRIEF (outward, extreme — identity fused to the bond) + FEAR (reflexive)` · *vs* **devotion**: devotion is chosen and coexists with an intact separate self.
- **Maternal love** — the intense, biologically primed bond of a parent toward offspring, combining nurturance, protectiveness, and a near-unconditional priority on the child's welfare. `CARE (outward) 0.70–0.97 + PANIC_GRIEF (outward) + RAGE (outward, latent, protective)`
- **Worship / idolisation** — adoration escalated past proportion, elevating the other to near-sacred status and suspending ordinary critical judgment of them. `SEEKING (outward) 0.85–0.97 + CARE (outward)` · *vs* **adoration**: worship flattens the other into a symbol; adoration still sees the actual person.

### The pity block ★

*Entirely missing from the sweep. All five differ in the rank relation between feeler and sufferer — which is exactly the appraisal the basis cannot represent (Part 2, Group D).*

- **Concern ★** — attentive, low-grade care about another's welfare, sufficient to keep checking but not to reorganise one's own plans. `CARE (outward) 0.20–0.60`
- **Sympathy ★** — feeling *for* someone in their trouble, from outside it: a recognition of their suffering that does not require sharing it. `CARE (outward) 0.25–0.60` · *vs* **empathy**: sympathy stays outside and looks in.
- **Pity ★** — sorrow at another's suffering that positions the sufferer as beneath one, and is therefore frequently unwelcome to receive. `CARE (outward) 0.25–0.65, with a downward rank relation` ⚠D · *vs* **compassion**: compassion levels the rank and moves toward help; pity can be entirely static and looks down.
- **Empathy (affective) ★** — the involuntary catching of another's state: feeling *what* they feel, at whatever intensity theirs is running. `CARE (outward) 0.20–0.80, magnitude borrowed from the other's state` ⚠F · *vs* **compassion**: empathy can flood and paralyse; compassion is oriented toward acting.
- **Solicitude ★** — anxious, hovering care that expresses itself as attentiveness to the other's comfort and small needs. `CARE (outward) 0.30–0.65 + FEAR (outward) low`
- **Compassion ★** — being moved by another's suffering, with the impulse to relieve it, and without the rank asymmetry pity carries. `CARE (outward) 0.35–0.80`
- **Mercy ★** — the withholding of a harm one has the standing and the right to inflict. `CARE (outward) 0.35–0.75 + RAGE (outward) deliberately released` ⚠H · *vs* **forgiveness**: forgiveness releases the anger; mercy may keep the anger and decline to act on it.
- **Magnanimity ★** — greatness of spirit toward a defeated or lesser party: generosity precisely where pettiness would be permitted. `CARE (outward) 0.30–0.70 + RAGE withheld` ⚠D/H

### The repair block

*Cross-listed from RAGE (reflexive) and PANIC_GRIEF (reflexive) — placed here because what makes each of these more than self-anger is the turn toward the injured party.*

- **Chastened (feeling)** — a subdued, corrected state after a rebuke or humbling consequence: residual guilt plus resolve not to repeat it. `CARE (reflexive) 0.30–0.55 + FEAR (outward) mild + SEEKING redirected toward correction` · *vs* **contrition**: being chastened is imposed from outside.
- **Guilt** — a painful sense of having violated a moral or relational standard through a specific act or omission, focused on the deed rather than the self, and characteristically motivating confession, apology, or repair. `CARE (outward, for the one harmed) 0.35–0.65 + RAGE (reflexive) + PANIC_GRIEF (outward) mild` · *vs* **shame**: guilt leaves the self intact and repairable; shame condemns the whole self and offers no route out.
- **Confession-pressure ★** — the mounting need to say the thing aloud, against one's own interest, purely to stop carrying it alone. `CARE (outward, toward the wronged) 0.35–0.75 + FEAR (outward, of the consequence) opposing it`
- **Contrition** — guilt or remorse oriented toward repentance and the seeking of forgiveness, typically addressed to an offended party or moral authority. `CARE (outward, toward repair) 0.40–0.70 + FEAR (outward, submission-flavoured)` · *vs* **remorse**: contrition moves toward someone; remorse can remain private and inert.
- **Self-abasement** — deliberate, sometimes ritualised or excessive lowering of oneself before another, to appease, atone, or demonstrate submission. `CARE (outward, appeasement) 0.50–0.80 + FEAR (outward, submission)` ⚠D

### Duty, oath, and the collective

- **Obligation** — a felt duty to act for another's benefit because of a role, promise, or relationship, independent of any debt owed. `CARE (outward, at cost to self) 0.15–0.55` ⚠H
- **Hospitality-obligation / guest-right ★** — the felt bindingness of having taken someone under your roof, or having eaten under theirs: a duty of protection that overrides prior enmity. `CARE (outward, role-derived) 0.25–0.60` ⚠H · *vs* **obligation**: guest-right is triggered by a threshold being crossed and is felt as sacred rather than contractual.
- **Solidarity (felt)** — a warm sense of shared cause and mutual loyalty with a group, especially against an outside pressure. `CARE (outward, toward the in-group) 0.25–0.70 + RAGE (outward, toward what threatens it)` ⚠A — the bound-together warmth itself is consummatory. · *vs* **camaraderie**: solidarity is defined by an adversary.
- **Fealty / troth ★** — the sworn bond: a commitment that has been *given* and therefore constrains, felt as binding whether or not affection survives. `CARE (outward) 0.40–0.85` ⚠H · *vs* **loyalty**: fealty was formally undertaken at a moment, and breaking it is a category of act, not a change of feeling.
- **Caregiver ambivalence ★** — the coexistence of genuine love for a dependent with the wish to be free of them, and self-condemnation for the second half. `CARE (outward) 0.30–0.70 + RAGE (outward, at the burden) + DISGUST (reflexive)`
- **Compassion fatigue ★** — the erosion of the capacity to feel for others through sustained exposure to their suffering; the care is intact as an intention and absent as a feeling. `CARE (outward) intact in intent, dropped to 0.00–0.20 in feeling` ⚠G/J
- **Callousness / hardness of heart ★** — the settled, sometimes deliberate suppression of CARE toward a class of person, permitting harm to them without disturbance. `CARE (outward) suppressed to near-zero` ⚠G/H · *vs* **cruelty**: callousness does not want the suffering; it simply does not register it as a claim.

### Partials

- **Kama muta / being moved ★** — the sudden lump-in-throat, chest-warmth, eye-prickle at a reunion, an act of unexpected kindness, or a bond suddenly intensifying. `CARE (outward) 0.45–0.85, abrupt intensification` ⚠A/E — the located half is the CARE surge; the *moved* quality needs both a consummatory reading and an expectation channel.
- **Moral elevation ★** — the warm, uplifting, faintly aspirational response to witnessing someone behave with unusual moral beauty. `CARE (outward) 0.40–0.80 + SEEKING (outward, wanting to be like that)` ⚠A · *vs* **admiration**: elevation is triggered specifically by virtue and produces a wish to be better rather than a wish to be near.

## CARE — REFLEXIVE

- **Sheepishness** — mild, submissive, often smiling embarrassment after a minor foolish act, combining apology with self-deprecating good humour. `CARE (reflexive) 0.25–0.45 + FEAR (outward) mild + PLAY (the rueful grin)`
- **Indebtedness** — the uneasy sense of owing a return for a benefit received, felt as an unpaid balance pressing for discharge. `CARE (reflexive, obligation to reciprocate) 0.25–0.65 + FEAR (unbound, of the social cost of defaulting)` ⚠C · *vs* **gratitude**: indebtedness is the discomfort of the balance; gratitude (unlocatable) is the warmth that persists when nothing is owed.
- **Self-compassion ★** — the extension to oneself of the care one would extend to another in the same trouble. `CARE (reflexive) 0.25–0.65` · *vs* **self-pity**: self-compassion acknowledges the pain and moves; self-pity dwells and wants rescue.

## CARE — UNBOUND

- **General benevolence ★** — diffuse goodwill toward whoever is in front of you, extended without particularity. `CARE (unbound) 0.20–0.60`
- **Piety ★** — the felt obligation and warmth owed to the gods, the dead, and the order of things: a devotion whose object cannot reciprocate in kind. `CARE (unbound) 0.30–0.80 + FEAR (outward, submissive)` ⚠H
- **Agape** — selfless universal benevolent love toward others or humanity in general, without requiring bond, familiarity, or reciprocity. `CARE (unbound) 0.40–0.80` · *vs* **philia**: agape needs no relationship history and can extend to strangers.

## PLAY

*Engagement for its own sake, without stake. Thin in the sweep; eleven additions below.*

- **Levity** — a lightness of manner or mood, especially one that treats a serious matter without due gravity: a tone rather than an activity. `PLAY (unbound) 0.00–0.25` · *vs* **playfulness**: levity is a tone, playfulness an orientation toward doing.
- **Whimsy ★** — a delight in the arbitrary and the slightly absurd, indulged because it pleases and for no other reason. `PLAY (unbound) 0.10–0.40`
- **Camaraderie** — easy, mutual goodwill among peers who share an activity, role, or experience, without a cause to defend. `PLAY (mutual, no stake) 0.15–0.55 + CARE (outward) light`
- **Teasing ★** — provocation offered as affection, calibrated to stay just inside what the target can absorb; the calibration is the care in it. `PLAY (outward) 0.20–0.55 + RAGE (outward) trace, defanged` · *vs* **mockery**: teasing is bounded by the bond and stops when it lands wrong; mockery aims to land wrong.
- **Playfulness** — a light, exploratory orientation toward activity or interaction, done for enjoyment rather than outcome, in a game-like spirit. `PLAY (unbound or outward, toward a partner) 0.25–0.55`
- **Amusement** — light, attentive pleasure in something entertaining or funny, engaging interest without requiring visible laughter. `PLAY (unbound-to-outward) 0.25–0.55, low commitment`
- **Banter / repartee ★** — competitive verbal play between equals, in which the contest is the pleasure and no one intends to win permanently. `PLAY (outward, mutual) 0.25–0.60`
- **Wit ★** — the pleasure of the mind moving quickly and precisely, taken in one's own facility as much as in the joke. `PLAY (outward/reflexive) 0.30–0.65`
- **Mischief ★** — the appetite for a small, reversible transgression carried out for the pleasure of the disruption. `PLAY (outward) 0.30–0.65 + RAGE (outward) trace` · *vs* **spite**: mischief's harm is meant to be recoverable and often shared afterward; spite's is not.
- **Gallows humour ★** — the joke made inside the disaster, which discharges the fear without denying it. `PLAY (outward) 0.35–0.75 with FEAR (outward) 0.55+ running underneath, unreduced`
- **The giggles / suppressed laughter ★** — helpless laughter arriving where it is forbidden, intensified by the prohibition. `PLAY (outward) 0.40–0.80, amplified by suppression`
- **Absurdist delight ★** — the specific pleasure of a thing being nonsensical: not funny *about* something, funny by being incoherent. `PLAY (unbound) 0.40–0.80`
- **Agonistic relish (competitive joy) ★** — the enjoyment of a contest as a contest: wanting a strong opponent rather than an easy win. `PLAY (outward) 0.45–0.85 + RAGE (outward, bounded by the rules)`
- **Mirth** — visible, sociable good humour and merriment, typically with laughter or smiling, and usually shared. `PLAY (outward, social) 0.55–0.80`
- **Glee** — lively, often mischievous or triumphant delight, frequently secretive or self-satisfied: the pleasure of getting away with something. `PLAY (outward) 0.55–0.80 + RAGE-tinged edge` ⚠A · *vs* **merriment**: glee can be solitary and covert.
- **Merriment** — cheerful festivity and lightheartedness as shared by a group on a social or celebratory occasion. `PLAY (outward, collective) 0.55–0.80`
- **Jollity** — full, hearty, festive good humour, especially the convivial cheer of holiday gatherings; somewhat old-fashioned. `PLAY (outward, festive) 0.55–0.80`
- **Ilinx (vertigo-play) ★** — the deliberate courting of disordered perception for pleasure: spinning, the drop, the drug, the dance to exhaustion. `PLAY (unbound) 0.55–0.90 + FEAR (outward) recruited as an ingredient`
- **Hilarity** — extreme, often uncontrollable mirth, expressed as convulsive or helpless laughter. `PLAY (outward) 0.80–0.95, at loss of control`

*Cross-references out:* mockery, ridicule, derision → DISGUST. Spite, cruelty, battle-joy → RAGE. Flirtation → LUST.

## DISGUST — OUTWARD

*Expel — this would contaminate me.*

**The rank sub-block.** Contempt through hubris are grouped here because DISGUST is the one primitive present across all of them, but every one of them is really a *rank* reading with disgust standing in. See Part 2, Group D — this block is the single largest piece of evidence for a rank comparator.

- **Squick** — a personal, idiosyncratic disgust at specific content that one does not judge wrong for others: *not to my taste* rather than a verdict on the object. `DISGUST (outward, narrow/personal) 0.10–0.25`
- **Distaste** — mild-to-moderate dislike or displeasure at something offensive to one's sensibility, taste, or manners, applied as readily to a shabby joke as to a bad meal. `DISGUST (outward) 0.10–0.30` · *vs* **aversion**: distaste is a passive judgment; aversion drives avoidance.
- **Condescension** — treating someone as an acknowledged inferior while maintaining a veneer of kindness or helpfulness: contempt wearing a polite mask. `DISGUST (outward, masked) 0.15–0.35 + RAGE (outward) mild, dominance` ⚠D *[Merged: the Disgust and Self-Conscious sweeps describe the same behaviour from either side.]* · *vs* **disdain**: condescension keeps engaging, helpfully; disdain withdraws.
- **Superiority (felt)** — a standing comparative sense of ranking above others: settled self-elevation rather than pride in an achievement. `DISGUST (outward, mild, toward those ranked below) 0.15–0.55, RAGE dormant` ⚠D
- **Queasiness** — an unsettled, churning stomach accompanying mild-to-moderate disgust: the body's early warning that this might be bad to take in. `DISGUST (outward/unbound, somatic) 0.20–0.40`
- **Superciliousness** — behaving as though habitually superior, most visible in a specific expressive register: the raised eyebrow, the appraising look, the faint smile of assumed advantage. `DISGUST (outward, mild) 0.20–0.40` ⚠D · *vs* **haughtiness**: superciliousness names the look; haughtiness the whole bearing.
- **Disdain** — cool, aloof dismissiveness treating a person or thing as unworthy even of the effort of anger, enacted through withdrawal rather than confrontation. `DISGUST (outward, low arousal) 0.20–0.50, RAGE largely absent` ⚠D *[Band reconciled across three sweeps.]*
- **Haughtiness** — pride and disdain held together: an air of superiority displayed openly in bearing, posture, and tone, needing no specific target to react to. `DISGUST (outward, mild) 0.25–0.45` ⚠D
- **Aversion** — strong dislike or reluctance producing an active urge to avoid, turn away from, or have nothing further to do with. `DISGUST (outward) 0.25–0.45, with a FEAR-bordering withdrawal`
- **Squeamishness** — a heightened, easily triggered sensitivity to disgust — gore, injury, insects, bodily functions — naming a low threshold rather than an episode. `DISGUST (outward) 0.25–0.50, dispositional`
- **Snobbery / snobbishness** — a chronic disposition to scorn those regarded as inferior in class, taste, wealth, or intellect, paired with eagerness to court those regarded as superior. `DISGUST (outward, mild) 0.30–0.55` ⚠D
- **Contempt** — the settled judgment that a person or thing is beneath consideration — worthless, low, unworthy of one's engagement — combining disgust at the object with an assumed superiority over it; characteristically cold rather than hot. `DISGUST (outward) 0.30–0.65 + RAGE (outward) low` ⚠D *[Merged across three sweeps; band reconciled from 0.25–0.70 spread. The Anger sweep's framing — anger capped by a judgment of unworthiness — is the same state read from the other side.]*
- **Prudishness / priggishness** — chronic, easily triggered moral disgust specifically at sexual content or perceived impropriety, with a readiness to be offended on others' behalf. `DISGUST (outward, moralised) 0.30–0.60, dispositional`
- **Out-group revulsion ★** — disgust attached to a category of person as such, in which their ordinary presence registers as contamination. `DISGUST (outward, categorical) 0.40–0.90` ⚠D · *vs* **misanthropy**: misanthropy dislikes people in general and makes no exception for the in-group.
- **Sneering / scoffing** — a brief sharp expression of contempt: a curled lip, a dismissive laugh, a short put-down. The compact behavioural marker rather than a sustained campaign. `DISGUST (outward) 0.40–0.65, acute + RAGE mild`
- **Mockery / ridicule** — making someone an object of amusement in order to belittle them: imitation, jokes, or exaggeration used as an instrument of contempt. `DISGUST (outward) 0.45–0.75 + PLAY (weaponised) + RAGE mild` · *vs* **derision**: mockery is the performance, derision the stance behind it.
- **Contumely** — insolent, humiliating treatment or language directed at another: contempt enacted as an injury done to someone. `DISGUST (outward) + RAGE (outward) 0.45–0.70`
- **Scorn** — contempt given active, pointed expression through harshness, a cutting remark, or visible mockery, aimed at making the target feel it. `DISGUST (outward) + RAGE (outward, expressive) 0.45–0.75` *[Band reconciled across three sweeps.]*
- **Gross-out ("the ick," "the willies")** — a sudden involuntary spike of disgust at something viscerally repellent — blood, insects, slime, decay — brief, reflexive, often vocalised. `DISGUST (outward, acute) 0.45–0.70`
- **Arrogance** — a standing interpersonal stance of assumed superiority, expressed in how one treats others: presumptuous, dismissive of their standing or claims. `DISGUST (outward, dismissal) 0.50–0.80 + RAGE (outward, dominance assertion)` ⚠D · *vs* **hubris**: arrogance is the behaviour, hubris the internal overestimation.
- **Derision** — contemptuous ridicule, typically with laughter or jeering, treating its target as absurd and beneath serious engagement. `DISGUST (outward) 0.55–0.80 + RAGE (outward) + PLAY (weaponised)`
- **Disgust (core)** — strong, often visceral revulsion or disapproval at something unpleasant, offensive, or contaminating, carrying the urge to recoil from and expel the object. `DISGUST (outward) 0.55–0.75`
- **Nausea (as emotion)** — the felt sickness in stomach and throat riding along with intense disgust: the body registering something it must not take in. `DISGUST (outward/unbound, somatic) 0.55–0.80` · *vs* **revulsion**: nausea is the internal sensation, revulsion the whole-body recoil.
- **Moral disgust** — disgust extended from the physical to the ethical: revulsion at cruelty, betrayal, exploitation, or injustice, with the same urge to reject and distance. `DISGUST (outward) 0.55–0.85 + RAGE (outward) undertone` · *vs* **moral outrage**: moral disgust wants distance; outrage wants action taken.
- **Purity disgust (sacrilege-disgust)** — disgust at a perceived violation of purity, sanctity, or the natural order — desecration, taboo-breaking — which fires even where no one is harmed. `DISGUST (outward, sacred register) 0.55–0.85`
- **Repugnance** — a deep, almost principled distaste: the sense that something is so at odds with one's constitution or convictions that coexistence with it is intolerable. `DISGUST (outward, evaluative) 0.60–0.80`
- **Hubris** — inflated, global pride attributing success to one's fixed superior nature rather than to effort, with entitlement and dismissive disregard for others: the pride that precedes a fall. `DISGUST (outward, looking down) 0.60–0.90 + RAGE (outward, latent dominance)` ⚠D/A
- **Body-horror ★** — disgust at the body as body: its wrongness, its openings, its capacity to be opened, one's own included. `DISGUST (outward, or reflexive at one's own) 0.60–0.95 + FEAR (outward)`
- **Repulsion** — a forceful dislike that seems to emanate from the object and push the observer away. `DISGUST (outward) 0.75–0.90` · *vs* **revulsion**: repulsion describes the stimulus's push, revulsion the subject's recoil — the same transaction from either end.
- **Loathing** — intense, standing hatred fused with disgust, directed at someone found so repellent that their mere existence offends. `DISGUST (outward) 0.78–0.95 + RAGE (outward, low boil)` *[Bands reconciled: Anger 0.75–0.95, Disgust 0.80–0.95.]*
- **Revulsion** — a sudden strong access of disgust and horror, felt as a violent turning-away of the whole body and mind. `DISGUST (outward, acute) 0.80–0.95, bordering FEAR`
- **Detestation** — intense, settled hatred and dislike: a formal register for *I cannot stand this*, combining loathing's heat with a near-permanent verdict. `DISGUST (outward) + RAGE (outward) 0.85–0.97`
- **Abhorrence** — repugnance so complete the object is regarded as intrinsically detestable: a near-total shrinking-from, grounded in principle rather than personal animus. `DISGUST (outward) 0.90–1.00` · *vs* **detestation**: abhorrence stays disgust-forward and avoidant; detestation carries active hatred.
- **Execration** — the act or feeling of cursing and denouncing something as utterly vile: loathing that wants to speak itself aloud as condemnation. `DISGUST (outward) + RAGE (outward, verbal) 0.90–1.00`
- **Abomination (the felt judgment)** — regarding something as a violation so total that its very existence offends a sacred or natural order: not *I dislike this* but *this ought not to exist.* `DISGUST (outward, categorical, moralised) 0.95–1.00`

## DISGUST — REFLEXIVE

- **Self-deprecation** — a habitual tendency to undervalue, criticise, or make light of oneself, whether from genuine low self-regard or as a social and defensive style. `DISGUST (reflexive) 0.15–0.40 + PLAY (when humorous)` · *vs* **self-contempt**: self-deprecation is verbal, social, and often light; self-contempt is private and cold.
- **Cringe (retro-cringe) ★** — the involuntary full-body wince at the memory of one's own past self: the thing said at nineteen, arriving unbidden at three in the morning. `DISGUST (reflexive, at a former self) 0.25–0.60` ⚠F · *vs* **shame**: cringe needs no current audience and no current stake; the self it condemns is one you have already left.
- **Vicarious embarrassment (Fremdscham)** — embarrassment felt on another person's behalf for their gaffe or exposure, arriving through simulation even when they show no embarrassment themselves. `DISGUST (reflexive, borrowed by simulation) 0.25–0.50 + CARE (outward, empathic)` ⚠F
- **Self-contempt** — regarding oneself as worthless or beneath one's own respect: the cool, dismissive register of self-judgment. `DISGUST (reflexive, cool) 0.40–0.70` ⚠D · *vs* **self-loathing**: self-contempt is a cold dismissal; self-loathing is hot and consuming.
- **Pity-shame ★** — the particular humiliation of being pitied: the discovery that one has been reclassified downward by someone kind. `DISGUST (reflexive) 0.40–0.80 + RAGE (outward, at the pitier)` ⚠C/D
- **Self-disgust** — disgust turned on one's own actions, body, or character, typically after a specific act regarded as shameful or repellent. `DISGUST (reflexive) 0.50–0.80`
- **Indignity** — the felt violation of what one is owed as basic respect, often from impersonal circumstance — illness, poverty, bureaucratic handling — rather than from a cruel agent. `DISGUST (reflexive) 0.50–0.75 + RAGE (outward/unbound, at the circumstance)` ⚠D · *vs* **humiliation**: indignity needs no one to have done it on purpose.
- **Shame** — a painful global evaluation of the self as defective, bad, or unworthy, triggered by real or imagined exposure of that defect, with the characteristic urge to hide, shrink, or disappear. `DISGUST (reflexive, the self as contaminant) 0.55–0.85 + FEAR (outward, of others' judgment)` ⚠F — the defining feature, the self perceived as an object in another's mind, is not on the basis; the located reading undercounts what makes shame shame. *[Adjudicated: the Disgust sweep called it unlocatable, the Self-Conscious sweep located it. Located, with the residual named.]*
- **Loss of face** — shame at a public diminishment of one's standing within a specific network whose good opinion carries ongoing currency; a reputational and strategic register of shame. `DISGUST (reflexive) 0.55–0.80 + FEAR (outward, of status loss)` ⚠D/F · *vs* **humiliation**: loss of face can be calculated and spoken of in the third person — *he cannot afford to lose face here.*
- **Ignominy** — public disgrace and loss of honour attached to one's name, especially as a lasting condition rather than a passing feeling. `DISGUST (reflexive) 0.60–0.85 + FEAR (outward, of a permanent record)` ⚠F
- **Humiliation** — the searing pain of being forcibly lowered in status or exposed as inadequate before others, especially when one can neither retaliate nor escape. `DISGUST (reflexive) 0.70–0.95 + RAGE (outward, at the humiliator, typically blocked) + FEAR (outward)` ⚠C/D *[Merged across two sweeps.]* · *vs* **shame**: humiliation requires an external agent and witnesses.
- **Mortification** — acute, overwhelming shame from a specific, usually public humiliating incident, intense enough to feel like social death. `DISGUST (reflexive) 0.70–0.98 + FEAR (outward, extreme) + PANIC_GRIEF (loss of standing)` ⚠F
- **Abjection** — being cast down, degraded, or expelled from the social order: both a condition and the felt experience of having become what one's own psyche recoils from. `DISGUST (reflexive, self as expelled matter) 0.75–0.95` · *vs* **self-loathing**: abjection carries a felt expulsion from belonging or personhood itself.
- **Self-loathing** — intense, chronic hatred and disgust directed at oneself: a standing condemnation of one's own existence or character rather than a reaction to a single act. `DISGUST (reflexive) + RAGE (reflexive) 0.75–0.98` *[Merged across two sweeps.]*

## DISGUST — UNBOUND

*The world itself gone untouchable.*

- **Jadedness** — a blunted capacity to be moved at all, from overexposure: less a felt emotion than the numbed disposition an old one left behind. `DISGUST (unbound) 0.10–0.30 — but this is a dampening of reactivity, not an activation` ⚠G
- **World-weariness** — a weary low-grade distaste for existence or experience in general: nothing on offer seems worth the trouble, and the distaste attaches to no one object. `DISGUST (unbound) 0.20–0.45, chronic, low arousal` · *vs* **jadedness**: world-weariness still *registers* things as not-worth-it; jadedness is the prior desensitisation that stops them registering.
- **Cynicism** — a habitual distrustful disgust toward stated motives, ideals, and institutions: the standing suspicion that professed sincerity always covers self-interest. `DISGUST (unbound, at motives generically) 0.25–0.50` · *vs* **misanthropy**: cynicism doubts sincerity and can coexist with liking people.
- **Misanthropy** — general dislike, distrust, or contempt for humankind as such rather than for any particular person or act. `DISGUST (unbound, object generalised to people) 0.50–0.80`
- **Existential nausea** — a state in which existence itself feels contaminating, absurd, or unbearable, with no locatable target: the disgust primitive firing with nothing to expel. `DISGUST (unbound) 0.60–0.90, acute-to-crisis` · *vs* **world-weariness**: existential nausea is acute and closer to crisis.

# PART 2 — THE UNLOCATABLE PILE

**This is the finding.** 51 words could not be placed at all, and 60-odd more are placed only partially. They do not fail at random. They fail in **eleven clusters**, and the clusters point at specific missing machinery. Two additions would fix most of it.

### Summary of what is missing, by how much it would unblock

| # | Missing machinery | Kind of fix | Entries unblocked |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A** | **A consummatory primitive** — a want registering as MET | ninth primitive | **~50** |
| **D** | **A signed rank comparator** — self vs other, ± | appraisal dimension | **~24** |
| **C** | **An INBOUND direction** — being the object of another's primitive | fourth direction | **~16** |
| **E** | **An expectation-violation channel** — signed prediction error | appraisal dimension | **~15** |
| **F** | **A self-as-seen channel** — my model of your model of me | appraisal dimension | **~15** |
| **H** | **A volitional / commitment axis** — a stance held against the drive | orthogonal axis | **~15** |
| **G** | **A regime flag** — mobilised vs shutdown, not a magnitude | state flag | **~11** |
| **I** | **A fairness / desert comparator** — a norm ledger | appraisal dimension | **~9** |
| **B** | **A derivative notation** — d(primitive)/dt, the falling edge | notation, not a primitive | **~8** |
| **J** | **An energy / capacity axis** — how much drive is affordable | orthogonal axis | **~6** |
| **K** | **A somatic channel** — pain, sickness, intoxication | outside scope, but flag it | **~5** |

Two of these are structural additions to the basis (A, C). Five are appraisal dimensions that sit *beside* the primitives rather than among them (D, E, F, I, and arguably G). Two are axes orthogonal to magnitude (H, J). One is pure notation (B) and costs nothing.

## GROUP A — THE CONSUMMATORY HOLE

**What is missing:** a primitive that registers **A WANT HAVING BEEN MET.** The eight model appetite, withdrawal, tending, and expulsion. None of them can represent arrival. The Joy sweep flagged this and it turned out to be the largest single gap in the exercise: **~50 entries**, spanning five domains, all failing for exactly one reason.

**Why the workarounds don't work.** SEEKING at high magnitude is *more wanting*, not having. PLAY is engagement without stake, which is not the same as a stake discharged. CARE is giving, not receiving. A falling primitive (Group B) covers relief but not contentment. There is no combination of the eight that yields *enough*.

**Fully unlocatable (nothing on the basis at all):**

*The quiet register:* **Contentment** (settled satisfaction with things as they are, nothing more currently wanted; causeless and durable) · **Serenity** (stable undisturbed inner calm regardless of circumstance) · **Peace / peacefulness** (settled absence of conflict, especially following its resolution) · **Comfort** (soothed ease of body or mind at the relief of strain, want, or exposure — *also Group C*) · **Bliss** (calm complete happiness at peak intensity, untroubled and unagitated) · **Beatitude** (bliss under a religious frame) · **Felicity** (formal literary word for great happiness or aptness) · **Gladness** (light responsive pleasure at a specific piece of news or presence) · **Cheerfulness** (a standing disposition toward brightness of spirit) · **Coziness / hygge ★** (the pleasure of a small warm enclosure with the weather outside).

*The hedonic register:* **Pleasure** (the base positive quale) · **Delight** (vivid, often sudden pleasure at something charming or welcome) · **Gratification** (the *at last* of a built-up want being met) · **Satisfaction** (calm sense that a want or standard has been adequately met) · **Satiety / satiation** (an appetite fully met — the exact terminus of every entry in the desire family) · **Savouring ★** (attending deliberately to a pleasure while it is happening, to extend it) · **Afterglow ★** (the specific quiet after sexual satisfaction) · **Euphoria** (intense expansive wellbeing, often objectless or disproportionate) · **Ecstasy** (trance-like rapture in which the sense of self dissolves) · **Rapture** (being seized and carried away by intense joy in response to something outside oneself) · **Jouissance** (pleasure exceeding or transgressing the bounds of pleasure, often bound up with excess or pain).

*The triumph register:* **Elation** (high-spirited lift at a specific success) · **Jubilation** (open exultant collective rejoicing) · **Triumph** (having decisively prevailed, and the glow attached to it) · **Exultation** (the vivid vocal display of triumph) · **Cock-a-hoop** (boastful, crowing, almost childish public triumph) · **Fiero** (pride of personal triumph over a self-chosen difficulty, through one's own effort) · **Pride** (pleased sense of one's own worth or achievement, or of something identified with oneself) · **Self-satisfaction** (quiet contentment with one's own conduct, needing no audience) · **Smugness** (excessive self-satisfaction with a superior air — *the comparative half is locatable under DISGUST/RAGE; the satisfaction is not*) · **Schadenfreude** (pleasure at another's misfortune — *the rivalry is RAGE; the pleasure is not on the basis*) · **Vindication** (the satisfaction of being proven right after being doubted, blamed, or dismissed) · **Catharsis of anger** (the release and lightness after expressing pent-up anger — *also Group B*) · **Revenge-satisfaction** (the gratification once a vengeful goal is actually carried out).

*The bond register:* **Gratitude** (warm appreciative sense of being indebted to a benefactor, with the impulse to acknowledge or repay — *also Group C*) · **Belonging** (the secure sense of being a welcome, held member of a group — the exact consummatory mirror of PANIC_GRIEF) · **Security in a bond** (the standing background sense that a bond is stable and will not be withdrawn) · **Compersion** (vicarious warmth at a loved one's happiness with another person — structurally the positive counterpart of jealousy) · **Unconditional love** (love whose commitment does not fluctuate with the other's behaviour or reciprocation, and is not experienced as effortful) · **Absolution / being forgiven ★** (the specific release of having a debt cancelled by the one owed) · **Homecoming ★** (the bond-confirmation of return).

*The self register:* **Dignity** (self-worth holding steady under scrutiny or pressure — reads as the absence of a breach rather than a drive) · **Self-respect (at rest)** (the standing regard for oneself as deserving fair treatment; *its reactive form, indignation when violated, is locatable*) · **Honour (felt) ★** (the settled possession of good standing, as opposed to the dread of losing it).

*The epistemic and aesthetic register:* **Certainty** (the subjective sense of knowing, distinct from being correct) · **Epiphany / insight** (the sudden flash in which a search resolves all at once) · **Beauty / the aesthetic response** (the reward of something perceived as harmonious or striking — *the approach to it is SEEKING and PLAY; the payoff is not*).

*The bittersweet partials — half located, half not:* **Nostalgia** · **Bittersweetness of memory** (sadness and pleasure held simultaneously in recalling something loved and lost — the grief maps to PANIC_GRIEF, the *having loved* does not) · **Mono no aware** (the aching awareness of the transience of all things, felt because something is beautiful and passing) · **Nostalgia for a lost love** · **Trust** (the low-FEAR reading is real; the positive confidence is not).

**What the fix is.** Add a ninth primitive — call it **CONSUMMATION**, or SATIETY, or REST: *a want registering as met, at magnitude proportional to the want it discharges.* It needs the same three directions as the others. Outward consummation is gratitude and compersion (another's act met my want; their want was met). Reflexive is pride and self-satisfaction. Unbound is contentment and bliss. **This single addition would unblock ~50 entries** and would let the desire, joy, love, and grief families close their loops. Without it, a showrunner can write the buildup and cannot write the release: the actor must be told *the absence of the preceding pull*, not the state itself.

## GROUP B — THE DERIVATIVE HOLE (cheapest fix in the exercise)

**What is missing:** notation for **a primitive's rate of change**, especially a steep fall. Several "positive" states are not states at all — they are a high magnitude dropping, and the drop itself is what is felt.

**Entries:** **Relief** (the pleasant release when an anticipated harm is removed or averted — mechanically FEAR falling sharply from high) · **Catharsis of anger** (RAGE falling sharply after expression) · **Forgiveness** (RAGE de-escalating toward baseline by choice — *located in Part 1 as a trajectory*) · **Anticlimax ★** (prepared arousal discharging with nothing to spend itself on) · **Survivor's lightness ★** (the guilty buoyancy after a near miss) · **The lull after a fight ★** · **Vindication** (RAGE discharging on external recognition — *the discharge is Group B, the payoff is Group A*) · **Grief's attenuation** (the fact, central to the whole grief family, that PANIC_GRIEF *comes down*).

**What the fix is.** Not a primitive. A notation: allow an entry to specify **Δ** — `FEAR (outward) 0.85 → 0.10 over seconds` — and relief is fully expressible without any new primitive at all. **This unblocks ~8 entries for free** and it is worth doing *before* the consummatory primitive, because it cleanly separates two things the Joy sweep conflated: relief (a fall) and contentment (a state). They are not the same shape and should not be fixed by the same mechanism.

---

## GROUP C — THE MISSING DIRECTION: INBOUND

**What is missing:** the direction set is OUTWARD / REFLEXIVE / UNBOUND — all three describe where *my* primitive is aimed. There is no way to represent **being the object of someone else's.** The basis can say *I care for you*; it cannot say *I am cared for*.

**Entries needing it:** **Comfort** (being soothed — CARE inbound) · **Feeling loved / cherished ★** (CARE inbound, sustained) · **Being needed ★** (CARE inbound, as a role) · **Gratitude** (CARE inbound, acknowledged) · **Belonging** (bond-confirmation inbound) · **Security in a bond** · **Thirst (slang)** and **Vanity** (LUST/admiration inbound, wanted) · **Being desired ★** · **Being seen ★** (attention inbound, neutral valence) · **Being envied ★** (a real and distinct pleasure/discomfort) · **Being feared ★** (the villain's satisfaction, and the tyrant's) · **Being pitied → pity-shame ★** (CARE inbound, unwanted, with rank attached) · **Humiliation** (RAGE/contempt inbound, publicly) · **Intimidation** (RAGE inbound, credibly threatened) · **Indebtedness** (CARE inbound, unbalanced) · **Self-pity** (CARE inbound, self-supplied, and failing).

**What the fix is.** Add **INBOUND** as a fourth direction on every primitive. It is cheaper than a new primitive and unblocks ~16 entries, several of which — being desired, being feared, feeling loved — are load-bearing for whole genres. Note the interaction with Group A: *comfort* and *gratitude* need both an inbound direction **and** the consummatory primitive; inbound CARE explains where it comes from, consummation explains why it feels good.

---

## GROUP D — THE MISSING COMPARATOR: RANK

**What is missing:** a **signed rank relation** between self and other. The sweep repeatedly resorted to phrases like *"plus an unlocatable superiority appraisal"* — six times in the Disgust domain alone. DISGUST is doing the work and doing it badly: contempt is not really *this would contaminate me*, it is *this is beneath me*, and those are different.

**Entries:** **Contempt · Disdain · Condescension · Snobbery · Haughtiness · Superciliousness · Superiority (felt) · Arrogance · Hubris · Self-righteousness · Smugness · Scorn** (all rank-down) · **Inferiority (felt) · Submission · Deference · Respect · Reverence · Awe** (all rank-up, including awe's characteristic self-shrinking) · **Humiliation · Indignity · Loss of face · Dishonour-dread ★ · Pity-shame ★** (rank forcibly lowered) · **Pity ★ · Magnanimity ★ · Mercy ★** (rank-down but with CARE, not disgust — impossible to distinguish from contempt on the current basis, which is a serious defect) · **Envy · Conceit · Pride**.

**What the fix is.** A signed scalar, **RANK: −1.0 to +1.0**, sitting beside the primitives as an appraisal. It immediately does three things the basis currently cannot: separates *pity* from *contempt* (same rank sign, opposite primitive), separates *awe* from *fear* (same primitive, opposite rank direction — awe is FEAR with the self shrunk and the approach retained), and gives the whole self-conscious family the axis it has been borrowing DISGUST for. **~24 entries.**

---

## GROUP E — THE MISSING CHANNEL: EXPECTATION VIOLATION

**What is missing:** a **signed prediction-error channel**. Every surprise-family word in the sweep was forced onto FEAR, which is wrong in an obvious way: surprise is not withdrawal from anticipated harm, and a *pleasant* surprise is misfiled completely.

**Entries:** **Surprise · Startle · Astonishment · Amazement · Shock · Stupefaction · Déjà vu · Jamais vu ★ · The uncanny · Confusion · Bewilderment · Epiphany · Inspiration ★ · Disappointment ★ · Disillusionment ★ · Anticlimax ★.**

**What the fix is.** A channel with **magnitude** (how far off the prediction was) and **sign** (better or worse than expected), firing *before* valence is assigned. This is what makes surprise valence-neutral until appraised, which every account of surprise insists on and this basis cannot express. It also generates **disappointment** — which the ten-domain sweep missed entirely, and which is one of the most common emotions in fiction. **~15 entries.**

---

## GROUP F — THE MISSING CHANNEL: SELF-AS-SEEN

**What is missing:** a representation of **my model of your model of me.** The self-conscious emotions are not about the self; they are about the self *as an object in another mind*. The basis has no theory-of-mind slot, so shame reads as self-disgust plus fear, which undercounts it badly — you can feel self-disgust alone in a locked room, and it is not shame.

**Entries:** **Shame · Embarrassment · Mortification · Self-consciousness · Shyness · Abashment · Exposure · Exposure dread · Stage fright · Social anxiety · Loss of face · Ignominy · Vicarious embarrassment · Cringe ★ · Vanity · Disenfranchised grief** (the missing ingredient there is specifically *collective* recognition) · **Odium** (a reputational verdict held by others, not a feeling one person has).

**What the fix is.** A channel carrying **(imagined observer, imagined valuation of me)**. With it, shame = DISGUST reflexive *conditioned on* a negative imagined valuation; embarrassment = the same at low magnitude and local scope; mortification = the same with the observer real and present. Without it, all three collapse into one another. **~15 entries.**

---

## GROUP G — THE MISSING FLAG: SHUTDOWN REGIME

**What is missing:** the recognition that some states are **not points on a magnitude scale but a different operating regime.** The Fear sweep put this precisely: freeze is not "very high FEAR," it is what supersedes FEAR once fight and flight are judged futile, and the feeling flips from frantic to eerily flat rather than intensifying.

**Entries:** **Freeze / tonic immobility** (the parasympathetic shutdown past extreme fear: still, numb, often strangely calm or dissociated) · **Grief numbness** (blunted affect and unreality early in acute grief, buffering overwhelming pain) · **Shock** (partly) · **Stupefaction** (partly) · **Dissociation ★** · **Depersonalisation ★** (the self observed from outside) · **Derealisation ★** (the world behind glass) · **Jadedness** (chronic desensitisation) · **Anhedonia ★** · **Apathy ★** · **Emotional blunting ★** · **Callousness ★** (elective, toward one class of person) · **Compassion fatigue ★**.

**What the fix is.** A **regime flag: MOBILISED / SHUTDOWN**, orthogonal to magnitude, plus permission for **expressed magnitude to diverge from underlying magnitude**. Grief numbness is `PANIC_GRIEF underlying 0.90, expressed 0.10` — a state the current notation literally cannot write, and one that every bereavement scene needs. **~11 entries.**

---

## GROUP H — THE MISSING AXIS: WILL

**What is missing:** an axis for **a stance held against the drive reading.** The basis is entirely motivational: it says what pulls. It has no way to say what a character *decides* in the face of the pull. This is why the sweep produced **cowardice but no courage** — cowardice is fear winning, which is expressible; courage is fear present and overridden, which is not.

**Entries:** **Courage / bravery ★ · Sangfroid ★ · Equanimity ★ · Stoic acceptance / amor fati ★ · Resolve · Determination · Conviction · Resignation · Forgiveness · Mercy ★ · Loyalty · Fealty / troth ★ · Devotion · Obligation · Guest-right ★ · Scruple · Temperance / restraint ★ · Unconditional love · Vow-keeping ★ · Piety ★ · Callousness ★** (elective suppression).

**What the fix is.** A **COMMITMENT** axis: magnitude of a held stance, and what it is held *against*. Notation like `FEAR 0.85 / COMMITMENT 0.90 opposing` gives you courage, and `FEAR 0.85 / COMMITMENT 0.10` gives you cowardice, from the same fear reading — which is right, because the fear is the same and the man is different. **~15 entries**, and it is the axis that most changes what a showrunner can write, because character is largely made of it.

---

## GROUP I — THE MISSING COMPARATOR: FAIRNESS

**What is missing:** a **norm-violation ledger** — what is owed, by whom, and whether the account is settled. The Anger sweep wrote *"plus a fairness/desert appraisal (no dedicated primitive)"* and then simply proceeded.

**Entries:** **Indignation · Righteous indignation · Outrage · Resentment · Entitlement · Betrayal · Obligation-resentment ★ · Sense of injustice ★ · Gratitude** (partly — a benefit received *beyond* what was owed) · **Vindication** (the ledger being publicly corrected).

**What the fix is.** A **DESERT** appraisal: a signed ledger of what is owed to and by the self and others. It distinguishes anger-at-an-obstacle from anger-at-a-wrong, which are different states with the same RAGE reading, and it is what makes indignation available to a character who was not personally harmed. **~9 entries.**

---

## GROUP J — THE MISSING AXIS: ENERGY

**What is missing:** magnitude measures how strong a pull is, not how much **capacity** the organism has to act on it. "Too tired to be angry" is a real and common state and cannot be written.

**Entries:** **Exhaustion ★ · Listlessness ★ · Acedia ★ · Compassion fatigue ★ · Grumpiness** (which is *specifically* low-energy irritability) · **Hurry-sickness ★**.

**What the fix is.** An **ENERGY / CAPACITY** scalar modulating expressed magnitude. Cheap, and it fixes a class of scene — the argument that fizzles because both parties are too tired — that currently has no representation. **~6 entries.**

---

## GROUP K — OUTSIDE THE BASIS ENTIRELY (flagged, not fixed)

**Physical pain ★**, **sickness / malaise ★**, **intoxication ★**, **sleep deprivation ★**. These are not emotions and should not be primitives, but they dominate emotional state and a showrunner needs to pass them to an actor. Recommend a separate **BODY** slot rather than any attempt to locate them on the eight. *(Note also that hunger and thirst appear in the lexicon in their motivational sense only; their somatic sense belongs here.)*

---

## AND ONE STRUCTURAL NOTE ON DIRECTION

Three of the groups above (C, D, F) are all versions of one deeper absence: **the basis models an organism acting on a world, not an organism embedded among other minds that are also modelling it.** Rank, being-the-object-of, and self-as-seen are three faces of the same missing apparatus. If only one thing is added after the consummatory primitive, add a **social frame** carrying all three: `(other, rank±, their-model-of-me±, direction inbound/outward)`. That one structure closes Groups C, D, and F together — **~55 entries**, more than the consummatory primitive.

# PART 3 — WHAT THE SWEEP MISSED

Ten domains is a partition someone chose, and it leaked in six predictable places. **73 additions**, all located in Part 1 and marked ★. Here they are by why they were missed.

### (a) Whole regions of a primitive that no domain owned

The partition was organised by *English emotion words clustered by folk category*, not by primitive. So primitives with no folk category of their own came out starved.

- **The pity block (CARE outward) — 8 additions.** Pity, compassion, sympathy, empathy, concern, solicitude, mercy, magnanimity. Astonishingly, none of these appeared in 452 entries. The Love domain covered *bonds*, the Social domain covered *standing*, and feeling-for-a-stranger-in-trouble fell between them. This is the single largest omission in the sweep.
- **PLAY — 11 additions.** Whimsy, teasing, banter, wit, mischief, gallows humour, the giggles, absurdist delight, agonistic relish, ilinx, flirtation. The Joy domain covered PLAY only as *merriment*, missing everything sharp, competitive, verbal, or erotic. Flirtation and banter are both structurally central to fiction and both were absent.
- **FEAR reflexive — 5 additions.** Self-doubt, fear of one's own capacity, fear of losing control, l'appel du vide, and (from group G) the fear of one's own dissociation. The Fear domain swept fear-of-things thoroughly and fear-of-oneself not at all.
- **CARE's dark side — 3 additions.** Caregiver ambivalence, compassion fatigue, callousness. The Love domain was warm; the Disgust domain was cold; the place where care curdles or runs out was between them.
- **RAGE's appetitive and playful forms — 3 additions.** Cruelty/sadism, battle-joy, blood-feud obligation. The Anger domain treated rage as *reactive to a wrong*; rage as appetite and rage as inherited duty were missed.

### (b) Mixed and ambivalent states

The sweep's per-entry format encouraged single states with clean primitive readings. States whose *whole content* is a conflict got dropped.

**Morbid curiosity ★** (SEEKING toward what DISGUST rejects) · **Ruinous attraction ★** (LUST recruiting FEAR rather than opposing it) · **Shame-desire ★** (wanting what one is ashamed of, undiminished by the shame) · **L'appel du vide ★** · **Gallows humour ★** (PLAY running on top of undiminished FEAR) · **Caregiver ambivalence ★** · **Erotic tenderness ★** (LUST and CARE fused rather than alternating) · **Confession-pressure ★** (CARE toward the wronged pulling against FEAR of the consequence) · **Lítost ★** · **Sangfroid ★** · **Courage ★**. *Existing entries of this kind that the sweep did catch and should be read alongside them: love-hate ambivalence, betrayed love, bittersweetness, the sublime, temptation.*

**Note for the showrunner:** a mixed state should be written to the actor as **two live vectors, not one blended one.** "You want him and you are disgusted with yourself for it" plays; "you feel conflicted" does not.

### (c) Emotions about time

The sweep had *anticipation*, *dread*, *nostalgia* and *hope* and nothing else. Time is a whole axis and it produced eight misses.

**Impatience ★** (anger at rate) · **Completion-pressure / the Zeigarnik itch ★** (the unfinished thing not releasing you) · **Restlessness ★** · **Anticipatory savouring ★** · **Foreclosure grief ★** (mourning the narrowing of possibility itself, distinct from regretting one choice) · **Mid-life reckoning ★** · **Anniversary grief ★** (the calendar producing grief in a body that has not consulted the date) · **Grief-ambush ★** (the discontinuity is the point) · **Hurry-sickness ★** · **Jamais vu ★**. Add **Torschlusspanik** as a usable loanword for foreclosure grief in its panicked register — the closing-doors panic, felt as urgency rather than sorrow.

### (d) States that are the absence of an emotion

The basis is built to represent activation, so absence is systematically invisible. Every one of these is a Group G problem.

**Apathy ★** (nothing is wanted, including relief from that) · **Anhedonia ★** (the pursuit survives; the payoff does not) · **Emotional blunting ★** · **Dissociation ★** · **Depersonalisation ★** · **Derealisation ★** · **Callousness ★** · **Compassion fatigue ★** · **Exhaustion ★** · **Indifference ★** (the true zero — distinct from apathy, which is a drive that should be there and isn't) · **Equanimity ★** (chosen non-reactivity, distinct from serenity: equanimity is *held*, serenity is *given*) · **Fearlessness ★** (a low signal, as against courage's overridden high one). *The sweep did catch freeze, grief numbness, and jadedness — all three failed the same way.*

**Note for the showrunner:** absence states must be written as **what is missing that should be there**, with the missing thing named. "You are not sad about your mother; you notice that you are not sad, and that is the disturbing part."

### (e) Pre-industrial and period states

A modern list omits everything organised around honour, oath, omen, and the sacred. Fourteen additions.

**Numinous dread (mysterium tremendum) ★** · **Piety ★** · **Superstitious dread / dread of omen ★** · **Dishonour-dread ★** · **Blood-feud obligation ★** · **Fealty / troth ★** · **Hospitality-obligation / guest-right ★** · **Ancestor-piety / longing for the ancestral dead ★** · **Exile-grief / lordlessness ★** · **Acedia ★** · **Battle-joy / berserkergang ★** · **Weltschmerz ★** (Romantic-era, still usable) · **The felt weight of a curse ★** (FEAR outward, at a harm believed already set in motion and not yet arrived, 0.40–0.85) · **Fatalism / wyrd ★** (the settled sense that the outcome is already fixed and one's part is only to meet it well — `SEEKING withdrawn + COMMITMENT high`, ⚠H).

**Note:** several of these are not exotic. *Guest-right* and *blood-feud duty* are ordinary obligations felt with a bindingness a modern reader has to be shown; both need the COMMITMENT axis to write at all.

### (f) States English names only with a phrase

Not loanwords — states with no single word in any language on hand, that a novelist reaches for constantly.

- **The relief when a long-suffering relative finally dies, and the guilt attached to the relief ★** — `PANIC_GRIEF (outward) 0.55–0.85 + a sharp fall in FEAR/CARE-burden + DISGUST (reflexive) at the relief`. Group B and Group A both implicated. Common enough in fiction to deserve a coinage: **release-guilt**.
- **The dread of a pleasure you know will end, felt while it is still happening ★** — `SEEKING (outward) high + PANIC_GRIEF (outward) anticipatory`, 0.35–0.75. **Pre-emptive nostalgia.**
- **The collapse of desire at the moment of attainment ★** — `SEEKING dropping to near-zero at contact with the object`, Group A and B. **Attainment-flatness.** *Post-coital tristesse* is the special case.
- **Watching someone you love become someone you don't ★** — `PANIC_GRIEF (outward) chronic, at a person still present`. Close to ambiguous loss but slower and without its uncertainty: the loss is confirmed and ongoing. **Erosion-grief.**
- **The particular anger at being made to feel grateful ★** — located in Part 1 as **obligation-resentment**.
- **The shame of being pitied ★** — located as **pity-shame**.
- **The urge to say the damaging true thing ★** — located as **confession-pressure**.
- **The tenderness that only arrives after the argument ★** — `CARE (outward) 0.55–0.85, following RAGE's fall`. Group B. **Reconciliation-tenderness.**
- **Sonder ★** — the abrupt registering of a stranger as the protagonist of a life as detailed as your own. `SEEKING (outward) + CARE (outward), 0.30–0.60`, with a Group E jolt.
- **The pleasure of being needed ★** — Group C (inbound CARE) plus Group A. No word; universally understood.

# PART 4 — THE DEDUPE LEDGER

**Merged across domains** (one entry kept, best definition taken, bands reconciled): contempt (3), scorn (3), disdain (3), suspicion (3), reverence (3), gratitude (3), vindication (3), loathing (2), resentment (2), indignation (2), outrage + moral outrage (3), spite (2), vengefulness (2), envy (2), jealousy (2), possessiveness (2), yearning (3), longing (3), pining (2), wistfulness (2), sehnsucht (2), nostalgia (2), heartbreak (2), despair (2), regret (2), remorse (2), anguish (2), shock + grief shock (2), foreboding (2), dread (2), curiosity (2), chagrin (2), shame (2), self-loathing (2), self-contempt (2), mortification (2), humiliation (2), condescension (2), smugness (2), pride (2), schadenfreude (2), vigilance (2), trust (2), infatuation (2), crush (2), the creeps + the uncanny (2), estrangement ×3 → 1.

**Genuinely different things under one word — split:**
- **Awe / wonder.** The Joy sweep fused them; the Epistemic sweep is right to separate. Wonder leaves the self the same size; awe shrinks it. Both kept.
- **Ennui / world-weariness / jadedness.** Three sweeps produced overlapping words with genuinely different mechanics: ennui is withdrawn SEEKING, world-weariness is unbound DISGUST, jadedness is a dampening of reactivity. All three kept, with the distinctions stated.
- **Pride.** Splits four ways and each is a different state: achievement pride (unlocatable), hubris (located, rank-based), vanity (appearance, outward-seeking), conceit (importance, outward-seeking). All kept.
- **Self-respect.** Splits by whether it is at rest (unlocatable) or violated (RAGE, locatable). Both faces stated in one entry.

**Disagreements adjudicated (one line each):**
- **Anguish** — Fear sweep read it FEAR-first, Grief sweep PANIC_GRIEF-first. **PG wins:** anguish is suffering already underway, not harm anticipated.
- **Yearning** — split PG-first vs SEEKING-first across three domains. **SEEKING wins:** the forward reach is what makes it yearning rather than grief.
- **Shame** — Disgust sweep called it unlocatable, Self-Conscious sweep located it. **Located**, with the theory-of-mind residual named explicitly rather than swallowed.
- **Nostalgia** — Grief sweep located it, Love sweep called it unlocatable. **Both half-right:** the ache is located, the sweetness is Group A. Marked partial.
- **Jealousy** — Love sweep listed RAGE first, Social sweep FEAR first. **FEAR wins:** fear of loss is what distinguishes jealousy from envy; the rival-anger is downstream.
- **Reverence** — three domains, three placements. **SEEKING-dominant** by magnitude, with FEAR-submission as the differentia against admiration.
- **Contempt** — bands of 0.25–0.70 across three sweeps. **Set 0.30–0.65**; composition (DISGUST primary, RAGE low) was never in dispute.
- **Panic** — the Fear sweep's note that clinical panic may recruit PANIC_GRIEF circuitry is retained as a caveat, not adopted: **FEAR-dominant**, cross-primitive flagged.
- **Guilt vs remorse vs contrition** — the sweep split their dominant primitives inconsistently. **Settled as:** guilt = CARE-outward (repair-directed), remorse = PANIC_GRIEF-reflexive (inert grief), contrition = CARE-outward (addressed to someone). The differentia is *whether it moves toward anyone.*
- **Compulsion** — kept as its own entry under FEAR with the flag intact: it looks like desire and runs on relief-seeking, and misfiling it as SEEKING would give an actor the wrong instruction entirely.

## CLOSING NOTE FOR THE SHOWRUNNER

Three practical consequences of the above, for prompt-writing:

1. **When you need a positive state, you are on your own.** ~50 of the words a scene of happiness, triumph, or peace would want are not representable. Until a consummatory primitive exists, write these as **the drop** — *the fear you have carried for three weeks is gone; there is nothing in its place yet* — rather than as a state. That is Group B, and it is honest: it is what relief actually is.

2. **The rank words are lying to you.** Contempt, pity, awe, humiliation and shame all currently read as DISGUST, and they are not the same feeling. Until a rank comparator exists, **say the rank out loud in the prompt**: "he is beneath you and you do not intend to look at him again" and "he is suffering and you are above him and it moves you" are contempt and pity, and the primitive reading cannot tell them apart.

3. **Write the two vectors, not the blend.** The engine's magnitudes are per-primitive, and every good mixed-state prompt keeps them separate. Not *you feel conflicted* — **"Tom has humiliated you again, he is now smiling at you, you are in a blind rage, your only thought is violence"** works because it names one vector at 0.97 and gives it a target and an action tendency. For a mixed state, name two: *you want her, and you are disgusted with yourself for wanting her, and neither is winning.*
