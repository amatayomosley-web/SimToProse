# DISGUST — the band sets

**Status: DRAFT, for review.** Sixty bands at 0.05 steps. Rules as `docs/phrases-seeking.md`.

Regions (`docs/emotion-scales.md`): untroubled / distaste / aversion / revulsion / abhorrence.

**The only primitive needing all three tables** — it can be aimed at a thing, at yourself, and at
nothing in particular. It is also a deliberate departure from Panksepp, recorded in
`docs/emotion-basis.md` so it cannot rot back: he classed it a sensory affect, an exclusion on
circuit type rather than on irreducibility.

**The channel DISGUST owns**, for the composition rule: **the price of contact** — what may come
near, and what a case for nearness would have to weigh. Whether contact happens anyway belongs to the
actor, and to CARE.

---

## BOUND — something is what the distance is from

**The variable: how much distance you need.**

**The competing axis this set must NOT encode:** **danger.** Recoil from what it could do to you is
FEAR. Every band prices what contact would make of you, never harm.

**Mood:** contact-conditional — the contact never occurs in the phrase, it is priced ever higher.
The floor is habitual.

| band | phrase |
|---|---|
| 0.00 | nothing here is beneath touching, and the question does not arise |
| 0.05 | what is in reach is just what it is, and nothing in you sorts it |
| 0.10 | now and then something registers as a thing you would handle second, and that is all |
| 0.15 | there is a faint preference about what comes close, and it costs nothing to have |
| 0.20 | some things have started to carry a rather-not, and it stays faint |
| 0.25 | you would rather not touch it, and you could |
| 0.30 | being near it takes a small deliberateness that being near other things does not |
| 0.35 | what it has touched has begun to carry a little of it |
| 0.40 | the distance between you and it is a thing you know the size of |
| 0.45 | you can picture contact, and something in you leans away from the picture |
| 0.50 | where it has been matters now, as well as where it is |
| 0.55 | the distance is no longer keeping itself, and part of you has the keeping of it |
| 0.60 | what it touches, you are keeping track of, and the list does not get shorter |
| 0.65 | sharing anything with it has begun to cost, even the kinds of sharing that touch nothing |
| 0.70 | you are counting what stands between you and it, and wanting the count higher |
| 0.75 | its coming closer would need a reason, and the reasons are getting harder to imagine |
| 0.80 | contact would have to be justified to you, and nothing present is doing it |
| 0.85 | any reason for contact arrives already too small, whoever brings it |
| 0.90 | knowing it is near is itself a kind of touch now |
| 0.95 | there is no case for contact that you could hear as a case |

0.35 and 0.50 carry contagion — disgust's signature structure — framed as registration. 0.80 encodes
the normative aversion→revulsion boundary verbatim.

**Flagged trade at 0.55:** *"part of you has the keeping of it"* reports ongoing self-positioning —
actability chosen over strict felt-state, the same licensed form as FEAR 0.60.

## REFLEXIVE — you are what the distance protects them from

**What it is.** Not expel-it-from-me but **withhold-me-from-them**, rendered as felt state rather
than as the concealment acts the old phrases named.

**The variable: how much distance you need between you and them, with you as what the distance is
protecting them from.**

**The competing axis this set must NOT encode:** **loss.** Mourning a former self is reflexive
PANIC_GRIEF. This table is present-tense unfitness — no absence, no before-and-after of the self.

| band | phrase |
|---|---|
| 0.00 | your touch leaves things as they were, and it does not occur to you to check |
| 0.05 | what you bring into a room is only you, and that asks no checking |
| 0.10 | now and then something of yours feels like slightly too much to hand over, and the feeling passes |
| 0.15 | a faint check runs before you give any of yourself over, and it costs nothing |
| 0.20 | parts of you have started to feel like they need a once-over before company |
| 0.25 | you would rather they did not get all of you, and you could let them |
| 0.30 | being seen closely takes a small deliberateness, and being seen at a distance takes none |
| 0.35 | what you touch has started to seem like it keeps a little of you it did not ask for |
| 0.40 | how much of you they are getting is a thing you know the size of |
| 0.45 | you can picture being fully seen, and something in you leans away from the picture |
| 0.50 | where you have been close to people has started to matter, on their account |
| 0.55 | the amount of you that reaches them is being kept now, and you are the one keeping it |
| 0.60 | what you keep back, you are keeping track of, and the list does not get shorter |
| 0.65 | being included has begun to cost, even in the ways that involve no touching |
| 0.70 | every bit of you that shows has begun to feel like more than the room agreed to |
| 0.75 | your coming closer would need a reason, and you are running out of ones you believe |
| 0.80 | being received is a thing that would need justifying now, and nothing you have is doing it |
| 0.85 | any reason for having you near arrives already too small, and you are the one who sizes it |
| 0.90 | their knowing you are here has started to count as contact, and the contact is yours |
| 0.95 | there is no one your nearness would not be a thing done to |

**Replaced** the three existing reflexive phrases — *"you offer less of yourself"*, *"you keep
yourself out of what is offered"*, *"you take yourself out of the room"* — all three name acts. Their
content survives: withheld-quantity at 0.55–0.60, the sparing-them orientation at 0.50 and at the
ceiling.

Non-collision with reflexive PANIC_GRIEF holds band by band: **nothing here is gone, everything here
is too present.**

## UNBOUND — the distance is real and there is nothing it is from

**The variable: how much distance you need, with nothing it is from.**

**The competing axis this set must NOT encode:** **desolation.** A world gone untouchable must not
read as a world gone empty — that is PANIC_GRIEF's ceiling. Things stay full and present here, just
fouled-feeling. And per the recorded defect, no band may invent an object or a witness.

| band | phrase |
|---|---|
| 0.00 | everything is touchable, and touching it is not a subject |
| 0.05 | the world comes to your hand without being checked first |
| 0.10 | now and then a thing asks for a second look before it comes close, and passes |
| 0.15 | a faint sorting has started up in you, and nothing much is failing it |
| 0.20 | you are slightly harder to please about what comes close, and nothing in particular did it |
| 0.25 | you would rather not touch more than you must, and no one thing is why |
| 0.30 | the rather-not is general now, and it travels with you from room to room |
| 0.35 | what used to come to your hand now arrives with a question on it |
| 0.40 | how much space you have around you is a thing you know the size of |
| 0.45 | contact in general has started to need a small reason, and mostly there is one |
| 0.50 | no one thing deserves it, and everything is getting a little of it |
| 0.55 | the distance is no longer keeping itself, and there is nothing it is being kept from |
| 0.60 | the not-wanting-to-touch is plain in you, and it is not pretending to have a reason |
| 0.65 | sharing space at all has begun to cost, whatever is in the space |
| 0.70 | everything is dividing for you into touchable and not, and the second share is growing |
| 0.75 | whatever comes near would need a reason, and nothing that comes near brings one |
| 0.80 | any contact at all would have to be justified to you now, and nothing here is offering |
| 0.85 | any reason for touching anything arrives already too small, and it does not matter for what |
| 0.90 | everything reaches you as already handled, and by no one you could name |
| 0.95 | no distance you could get would be enough, and there is nothing it would be enough from |

**Replaced** the existing unbound phrase — *"you decline to touch it, and you do not disguise the
declining"* — an act plus a presentation choice. Its recorded insight was that the object phrase
*"invents a witness when there is none"*; that visibility-without-witness content lands at 0.60,
where the aversion is overt and unreasoned in the character and no witness is minted.

0.25 borrows the preserved LUST unbound construction (*"and no one thing is why"*) as the house form
for honest unboundness.

## Notes

* **Second-slowest decay in the basis (0.88)** — contempt is the durable social residue.
* **Ceiling composition.** Bound claims only the hearing-of-cases, and its hard pair is CARE — the
  nurse and the rot: *what you wanted for yourself is not in the calculation*, and *no case for
  contact reaches you as a case*, with the actor resolving which wins. Reflexive with CARE is the
  shame-driven caretaker. Unbound holds against PANIC_GRIEF's ceiling because distance needed from
  everything is not everything failing to add up.
