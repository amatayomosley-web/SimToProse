# Present Systems & State — step 4 (the operating present)

> The world's live "character sheet" — history's output, the sim's input; the source of every circumstance + consequence. Designed by **decomposing into its component systems, in dependency order.** This guide details the **legal** and **custom** presets (agnostic, multi-culture) — the load-bearing pair; **economy, factions, and the world-state ledger** follow. Rubric, not content.

## The components + their order (settled)
**economy + culture** (parallel foundations) → **governance / law-as-enforced** → **factions** → **world-state ledger.** De-duped (custom ⊂ culture; law + governance = one component). **Law derives from culture + economy + power — never authored law-first.**

## Agnostic by design — schema + preset library, instantiated PER CULTURE
Two principles carried from the character model:
- **Agnostic schema** = the content-free *dimensions* a system fills — not "here are the laws," but "here's what any legal/custom system must specify." (Same shape as the worth menu vs a person's weighting.)
- **Preset library (archetypes)** = reusable templates you **assign + perturb** per culture (exactly the `character-model.md` model pattern). Fast instantiation; a candidate set to curate, not canon.
- **Per culture, not per world.** A world holds *multiple* cultures/peoples; each gets its own custom instance (and its own or a shared legal instance). **The world is the *set* of them + how they contact.**

## The specials hook — what the planet & law make special for these people
An agnostic preset is universal *structure*; this world's *instance* is non-generic because the planet and universal law made things **special for these people**. Every preset carries a **specials hook** that injects them — and it's how present-systems satisfies the **inherit-upstream floor** (a generic economy in a magic world would *violate* the law).

**Mechanism — specials are sparse diffs over the agnostic schema** (the `character-model.md` archetype pattern, but sourced from *upstream* not authored): for each thing **law granted existence** (magic, gods, monsters, death-rules) or **the planet gave presence** (a unique resource, a singular biome), apply its **role** here. This is the placement rule's *role* tier — *existence @ law + presence @ planet → role @ present-systems*. A special does one of three things to the schema:
- **Breaks** an assumption (magic breaks production-needs-labor; undead breaks labor-needs-the-living);
- **Adds** a dimension or factor (mana as a factor of production; a magical material as a new scarcity axis);
- **Creates** an institution (a mages'-guild monopoly; a temple controlling a sacred resource).

Applies to **every** preset below — legal (laws about magic-use, supernatural crimes), custom (magic-user castes, rituals tied to *real* metaphysics), economy (worked through under its section). The agnostic schema is the base; the specials are the diffs that make it *this* world's.

## The Legal preset (law-as-enforced)
**Agnostic schema — the dimensions every legal system fills:**

| Dimension | The question | Ties |
|---|---|---|
| **Source** | where law's authority comes from — divine / sovereign / tradition / code / consensus | divine source is *literal* only if universal-law made the religion true; else *claimed* |
| **Legislator** | who makes/changes it — god-priesthood / monarch / elders / assembly / judges (precedent) | |
| **Enforcer** | who applies force — guards / courts / mob / kin-feud / none | |
| **Scope** | what it governs — property · violence · contract · family/marriage · status/caste · worship · speech | |
| **Adjudication** | how guilt/right is decided — evidence / ordeal / oath / status / combat / oracle | |
| **Sanction** | the consequence — fine · corporal · exile · death · enslavement · shame · restitution | |
| **Reach (stratification)** | applies *equally*, or differently by class / caste / gender / people? | **the multi-culture hinge** — unequal law across groups is dramatic fuel |
| **Law ↔ custom** | does formal law *override* custom, *defer* to it, or *coexist* (legal pluralism)? | the seam to the custom preset |

**Preset archetypes (assign + perturb):** theocratic-code · imperial-statute · feudal-obligation · tribal-elder-law · common-law-precedent · clan-feud/honor (no central law) · mercantile-contract.

## The Custom preset (culture-as-lived)
**Agnostic schema — the dimensions every culture fills:**

| Dimension | The question | Ties |
|---|---|---|
| **Values-weighting** | which of the worth-menu (Schwartz / Moral Foundations) this culture elevates | **a culture *is* a shared weighting over the worth menu — the collective analog of a character's Model** |
| **Kinship** | who's family · marriage rules · inheritance · lineage (patri/matrilineal) · household form | |
| **Status markers** | what confers standing — birth / wealth / age / piety / skill / honor — and how it's signaled | |
| **Taboo / sacred** | what's forbidden, what's inviolable | sanctity often → law (Source/Scope) |
| **Ritual / observance** | the practices that bind the group — rites of passage, worship, festivals | |
| **In-group / out-group** | who's "us"; how outsiders are met (hospitality ↔ hostility) | **the multi-culture hinge** — how this culture treats *other* cultures |
| **Gender / roles** | expected roles and their rigidity | |
| **Etiquette** | everyday surface norms — greeting, deference, speech register | read by observers (the character surface layer) |

**Preset archetypes (assign + perturb):** honor · dignity · face · clan/tribal · caste-stratified · egalitarian-communal · mercantile-individualist · theocratic-communal.

## The Economy preset (the material base — a foundation alongside culture)
**Agnostic schema — the dimensions every economy fills** (from "what drives an economy": scarcity + surplus are the twin engines):

| Dimension | The question | Ties |
|---|---|---|
| **Scarcity profile** | what's scarce, and where | **inherited from the planet's resource-spread** — the engine + the conflict substrate |
| **Factors** | resources (land/raw) · labor (people+skills) · capital/tech (tools, methods) available + distributed | |
| **Surplus** | subsistence ↔ high-surplus | sets social complexity — no surplus = no specialists/cities/ruling class |
| **Production mode** | how things are made — subsistence-farm / artisan / industrial / (magical) + tech level | |
| **Property / ownership** | who owns the factors and *captures the surplus* — communal/feudal/private/state/temple | → creates class (rich/poor) |
| **Labor organization** | who works, on what terms — free/serf/slave/guild/wage/caste | ties law (slavery is legal) + culture (caste) |
| **Exchange** | medium — barter/currency/market/gift/command — + **networks** (trade routes) | networks gated by the planet's geography/barriers |
| **Demand** | what's wanted — subsistence / status / ritual | ← culture's values + survival needs |
| **Control / extraction** | who taxes, regulates, captures | the seam to governance/law |

**Preset archetypes (assign + perturb):** subsistence-agrarian · feudal-extraction · mercantile-market · command-redistribution · guild-craft · (magical-production, if law permits).

**Specials (upstream diffs, per the hook):**
- **Magic** (law) → *breaks* production-needs-labor · *adds* mana-as-factor + magical-material scarcity · *creates* a mages'-guild monopoly.
- **Unique planetary resource** (planet) → the monopoly good everyone wants; control = whoever holds the deposit; the economy organizes around it.
- **Death-not-final / undead** (law) → *breaks* labor's mortality cost (undead labor); warps labor-organization + demand.
- **Real gods** (law) → temple captures surplus as tithe with *literal* divine backing; ritual demand has real effect.

**Dramatic framing (depth rule):** model to *circumstance-depth*, not a textbook simulation. The three drivers that generate drama: **what's scarce** (the director's levers), **who controls the surplus** (class → factions), **who depends on whom** (trade leverage — embargo, monopoly, debt).

## Multi-culture — the world is the SET of cultures + how they contact
A world holds **N cultures** (each a custom-instance, each with its own or a shared legal-instance). The load-bearing part is the **contact layer** — where cultures meet is where the drama is:
- **Dominance structure** — peers, or a *dominant* culture imposing its law/custom on subordinate peoples (empire over subjects)?
- **Legal pluralism** — does each culture keep its own law (dominant law *defers* to local custom), or is one law *imposed* on all? *(A minority's sacred rite that's illegal under the dominant law is pure dramatic fuel.)*
- **Friction points** — where values / taboos / laws **clash** (one culture's custom is another's taboo; the dominant law criminalizes a minority's rite).

**Friction points are not decoration — they are the inputs to two consumers:**
1. **Factions** (next component) — organized groups form along these fault lines.
2. **Character positions** (Phase B) — which culture/class a character is born into sets their formative baseline (inherited values-weighting, the law they live under, the out-group distrust a subjugated people carries). **Multi-culture present-systems *is* the space of positions a character can occupy** (`world-model.md` formative coupling, generalized from "the streets" to "the people").

## The Factions preset (the present's tensions, organized)
Factions are where the world's **fault lines become agents** — the organized groups that form along the economy's class lines, the multi-culture frictions, and the governance power structure, and *act* on them. They're the **convergence** of step 4 (they inherit all of it) and the **director's primary circumstance-source**: a faction's move *is* the circumstance placed before characters. Structurally, **a faction is a collective character** — interest = its goal, inter-faction relations = its relationships, resources = its capability (the same self-similarity as *culture = collective Model*).

**Agnostic schema — the dimensions every faction fills:**

| Dimension | The question | Ties |
|---|---|---|
| **Identity (fault-line)** | what binds them — class / people-culture / religion / region / profession / kin / ideology | the fault line is *inherited*: class ← economy, culture ← multi-culture, power ← governance |
| **Interest** | what they want / their grievance — surplus, autonomy, power, survival, restoration, a sacred aim | the faction's collective *drive* (social analog of a character goal) |
| **Resources (leverage)** | what they bring to bear — wealth / arms / numbers / legitimacy / knowledge-magic / a chokepoint | wealth ← economy, arms ← force, legitimacy ← law/divine, magic ← specials |
| **Status (position)** | dominant / subordinate / rising / declining / suppressed / outlawed | from governance + history; sets posture (defend ↔ overthrow) |
| **Inter-faction relations** | ally / rival / patron-client / open-enemy / uneasy-truce | **the tension structure** — factions are defined by their oppositions; this is the fuel |
| **Cohesion** | unified ↔ fractured (its own internal moderates vs radicals) | a fracturing faction is dramatic fuel |
| **Methods** | how they pursue it — political / economic / violent-insurgent / religious / covert | bounded by law + their resources |
| **Leadership / structure** | who leads, how organized — figurehead / council / hidden-hand / decentralized | the decision-locus (and a lever: co-opt or remove the leader) |

**Preset archetypes (assign + perturb into a contending *ensemble*):** ruling-elite · rising-merchant-class · suppressed-people/minority · religious-order/church · warrior-caste/military · criminal-underworld · reformers/revolutionaries · old-guard/restorationists · guild/professional-bloc.

**Specials (upstream diffs):** magic → a mages'-bloc as a power faction; real gods → the church wields *literal* divine legitimacy; undead/death-rules → a faction of, or commanding, the dead; a unique resource → the bloc controlling the monopoly.

**Two structural truths:**
- **Factions are history's live tensions, *organized*.** History's rule 7 ("end on live tension") loads the present with unresolved tensions; factions are the organizational bodies those tensions take. They inherit directly from history's output.
- **Factions are the bridge to the characters.** A character's *position* (Phase B) includes faction membership — it shapes loyalties, formative world, who they trust. A principal may *be* a faction leader, or be caught *between* factions. Factions are where society meets the individual.

**Dramatic framing (depth rule):** factions are the director's primary lever-source — model only the ones the story touches (the principals' factions + their opponents), to circumstance-depth. The load-bearing field is **inter-faction relations**: a faction with no opposition is inert; the *oppositions* are the circumstance fuel. Author the contending ensemble, not a census of every guild.

## Discipline (prime / craft / floor + depth)
- **Prime — curate to the premise:** instantiate only the cultures + legal/custom detail the premise levers. A two-culture story authors two; don't build a continent of peoples.
- **Floor:** inherit upstream (law's metaphysics, the planet, history) + no-contradiction across cultures and the ledger.
- **Depth:** archetype + perturb for background cultures; full schema-fill only for the cultures the story lives in.
- **Not exhaustive:** the 8+8 dimensions cover the common case; a premise can demand an axis no list anticipated — the meta-rules (default mundane, premise-justifies, depth) absorb it, same as the law guide's "on completeness."

## The "state" half — the world-state ledger
The five sections above are present-systems' **"systems"** (agnostic social schemas, authored at creation). The **"state"** half — the live *now* — is the **world-state ledger**, designed in its own doc: `world-state-ledger.md`. It's structurally different (runtime machinery — event log + folded snapshot + the write/read logic — not a social preset), and its line items are *runtime builds*, not authored content. **With it, step 4 — and the entire world half — is complete.**
