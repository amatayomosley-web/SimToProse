# FEAR — the band set

**Status: DRAFT, for review.** Twenty bands at 0.05 steps. Rules as `docs/phrases-seeking.md`.

**The variable: how much of your capacity is spent on what might go wrong.**

**The competing axis this set must NOT encode:** **activity.** A frightened person may freeze or move; the bands must not encode which, or a reader keys on agitation instead of capacity.

Regions (`docs/emotion-scales.md`): unguarded / caution / wariness / dread / terror

| band | phrase |
|---|---|
| 0.00 | the room reads as what it looks like, and checking does not occur to you |
| 0.05 | you are not counting anything |
| 0.10 | you register the doors the way you register the furniture |
| 0.15 | you know which way you came in |
| 0.20 | you have noticed where you would go, and it cost you nothing |
| 0.25 | the ways out are accounted for, and keeping them so costs you nothing |
| 0.30 | part of you is listening past the conversation for the wrong kind of sound |
| 0.35 | you would rather nothing were behind you |
| 0.40 | you are running the room for what could go wrong in it, and the running takes something |
| 0.45 | you are half a step behind your own sentences, and the missing half is out watching for harm |
| 0.50 | you are checking things twice against what could go wrong, and not deciding to |
| 0.55 | you would rather someone else committed first |
| 0.60 | you are choosing what to say by what it might cost |
| 0.65 | the ordinary business has begun to feel like looking away |
| 0.70 | you cannot put the danger down long enough to finish a thought |
| 0.75 | everything you do is arranged now around what it could do to you |
| 0.80 | watching for it has crowded out what you came here to do |
| 0.85 | next to it, what you came here to do has stopped being real |
| 0.90 | the rest of your life has gone flat and far away, and it alone is at full size |
| 0.95 | whatever else is in you, the danger has the use of it |

## Notes

* Fastest decay in the basis (0.72). A fear that outlives its cause belongs to `wound.py`, not here.
* The ceiling is capacity exhausted, not panic. A person at 0.95 may be perfectly still.
