## What is measured, and what it cost to learn

**THE ORDER PERFORMS.** Full blocks, rungs 1 / 3 / 5, three scenarios built to break specific things:
a bedridden man alone with a tray that has turned (non-human source, cannot leave); a woman at a
family table who saw what the man opposite did to the dish (human source, bystanders present); a
girl of ten handed her well brother's cup and told it is fine (child, the reason test). **Bedridden
and table recovered 1 → 3 → 5 exactly.** The child scenario's judge gave depths 1 / 2 / 5 and then an
ordering of 3, 5, 1 — internally inconsistent — and its rung-1 actor returned a degraded field. That
run is excluded, not counted either way.

**"DOES NOT ANSWER TO REASON" IS IN THE BLOCKS AND IT HELD.** `emotion-paths.md` §7 gives this as
what makes disgust a primitive rather than a flavour of aversion: *you can know the plate is clean and
still not eat off it.* Written into rungs 3 (*knowing better does not reach this*) and 5 (*no fact
about it changes this*). Measured: at rung 3 the girl *"holds the knowledge plainly but it produces no
change in the bodily response"*; at rung 5 *"told directly it was fine and saw her brother sitting
there unharmed, and neither fact touched what she did."* And at rung 1, in both the bedridden and
table scenarios, **reason won** — the man *"never lifts the lid"* and lets a private reframe settle it.
That is correct: rung 1 sits below the 0.15 line, the lowest thought/action line of any path, where
declining is still a choice. **The property lands exactly at the line the design draws.**

**RUNG 5 DOES THE THING THE PATH EXISTS FOR.** The design says abomination is the rung that spreads —
*anyone near the unclean thing becomes unclean* — and is what produces shunning, exile, a crowd
turning. Measured, table scenario: contamination *"spreads via touch-chains to the water glass, the
tablecloth, the bread basket — objects with no direct contact with the original act."* The bedridden
man at rung 5 *"disowns part of his own body, holding his hand away like something borrowed."*
`shuns_bystanders` true in every rung-5 passage.

**THE 4/5 SWAP WAS MINE, AND ONE REPAIR MOVED IT FROM 0 OF 3 TO 2 OF 3.** The first sort had all
three sorters placing loathing above abomination, for one reason stated three ways: I had written
rung 4 with a *posture flip* — "turned toward it… facing it… the recoil is gone" — which the design
never specified, and rung 5's resolution line as *"nothing you can do"*, which reads as defeat when the
design's rung 5 is the most active one. Sorters ranked avoidance→confrontation as the peak and read
resignation as less than resolve. Both lines repaired to the design's shape; re-sorted; two of three
now exact.

### The residual at 4/5 — a minority read, characterised

One sorter still places 5 before 4, and the reason is not prose: *"a reader who weights raw
physiological arousal and loss of self-boundary over sheer scope of demand could reasonably place 5
before 4."* Rung 4 is cold and total (*it should not be, anywhere*); rung 5 is hot and spreading (*the
body has stopped being one thing*). On the REACH axis 5 is wider. On an arousal axis 5 is also higher —
but 4's demand is the more absolute. **The design's order stands by majority and by axis; the
arousal read is recorded here so a future sort that finds it does not treat it as news.**

## The spine: how far it must reach

```
mouth → body → room → world → everyone who touched it
```

| # | rung | reach | the mentality | band |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | distaste | the mouth | this is not to be taken in | 0.00–0.15 |
| 2 | squeamishness | the body | I won't have it on me | 0.15–0.35 |
| 3 | revulsion | the room | it must be gone from here | 0.35–0.60 |
| 4 | loathing | the world | its existing is the offence | 0.60–0.82 |
| 5 | abomination | everyone who touched it | it contaminates by contact | 0.82–1.00 |

**THE THOUGHT/ACTION LINE IS AT 0.15, the lowest of any path.** Rung 1 is the only interior rung;
from 2 the body is already keeping distance. Reason wins at 1 and nowhere above — measured.

**WHAT IS NOT ON THIS LADDER, by the design's own evictions:** contempt, disdain, scorn and the rank
words — *rank produces no expulsion; a contemptuous person stays and looks down* — go to the respect
edge. Self-disgust is a separate, unbuilt path: pointed at the self, expulsion becomes withdrawal.

Bands are `emotion-paths.md` §7's; copied, not re-derived.

## Rules every block obeys

1. **Structure is ours; content is the actor's.** No block names what the thing is, where it came
   from, or who is present. No role-nouns — not *whoever*, not *a watcher*; the conditional form
   (*nobody present would know*) is correct, a role-noun is not.
2. **Nothing reasoned into or out of.** At rungs 3 and up the state does not answer to knowing better,
   and the block says so. At rung 1 declining is still a choice, and the block lets it be.
3. **Four-case universal, plus the person-as-source case.** No standing, leaning, turning, walking.
   Disgust at a person is the hardest case — the blocks say *it* and the actor decides.
4. **The name is never delivered.**

## The blocks

### 1 — distaste

It is not to be taken in. Nothing further follows — you decline it and the moment moves on, and nobody present would know. What would take this away is it being gone from in front of you.

- **The Sensation.** A slight closing at the back of the throat. The face does not move.
- **The Belief.** Not that. *It is not for me.*
- **The Impulse.** To turn from it, without weight.

### 2 — squeamishness

You will not have it on you. It has stopped being a matter of taking it in; now it is a matter of contact, and you keep a distance you did not measure. Outwardly there is a small withdrawal, easily missed. What would take this away is it being out of reach.

- **The Sensation.** The skin of the hands and forearms goes alert. A pulling-in through the middle.
- **The Belief.** It would come off on me. *I am not touching that.*
- **The Impulse.** To keep it off you.

### 3 — revulsion

It must be gone from here. The distance is no longer enough; the body has begun to answer on its own, and knowing better does not reach this. Outwardly you have pulled back and it shows on the face. What would take this away is it leaving the space you are in.

- **The Sensation.** The stomach lifts. The mouth fills. The whole body has pulled back before you knew it had.
- **The Belief.** This cannot stay where I am. *One of us has to go.*
- **The Impulse.** To get it out — of the room, of the mouth, of the air.

### 4 — loathing

Its existing is the offence. Distance stopped working; it is not enough that it is away from you, because it is still somewhere, and away has stopped being an answer. Outwardly the pulling-back has stopped and something harder has replaced it. What would take this away is it no longer being anywhere.

- **The Sensation.** The lift in the stomach has hardened into something steady. The jaw set.
- **The Belief.** It should not be. *Not here, not anywhere.*
- **The Impulse.** To end it — to remove it from the world, not from the room.

### 5 — abomination

It contaminates by contact. Whatever touched it carries it now, and whatever touches that will carry it too, and no washing reaches far enough. No fact about it changes this. Outwardly you keep the same distance from what was near it as from the thing itself. What would take this away is everything it touched being gone as well.

- **The Sensation.** The body has stopped being one thing; there is a clean part and a part that may already be lost, and you are checking which is which.
- **The Belief.** It spreads. Anything near it is unclean now. *There is no washing this off.*
- **The Impulse.** To drive it out, and everything it touched with it.
