## THE AXIS, AND WHY THIS PATH MAY NEED THREE VARIABLES NOT TWO

  "how much of you the wanting has taken" (docs/emotion-paths.md section 1)

  ATTENTION (1-4)   1 registered, nothing follows -> 2 want to KNOW more (not to have) -> 3 turned

  TEMPO (5-7)       5 SPIKES and will pass if you wait -> 6 no longer spikes, it RUNS, and can still

  YOUR LIFE (8-11)  8 your day has arranged itself around it -> 9 its ABSENCE is what you feel ->

Every path tested today needed TWO graded variables either side of a hinge. STIRRING looks like it
needs THREE - what of your ATTENTION it has, what its TEMPORAL character is, and what of your LIFE
it has. If so this extends the hinge finding rather than repeating it.

## 2026-09-08 - FOUR-CASE UNIVERSALITY BATTERY (rungs 7 and 11)

Cases: bedridden elder / ten-year-old / someone entirely alone / a cause that is not a person.

RUNG 7 ("it does not pass... other things are done AROUND IT, NOT INSTEAD OF IT") - 4/4 PASS.
  elder  "Everything else in this room can wait - the water going warm, the light moving off the
         wall, whatever she's doing down there with the radio on... Not later, not when the tray
         comes, not talked about. IN MY HANDS."
  child  CLEANEST INSTANCE. "My hands keep finding things to do - the loose thread on my sleeve, the
         zip, the loose thread again - AND NONE OF IT IS THE THING." Then positioning: "I go and
         stand near them, not touching, just near, so that when someone finally turns around I'm
         already there and it's already almost happening."
  alone  "I pick up the mug, find it cold, set it down without drinking. I straighten the papers
         into a stack that did not need straightening, then unstack them." And the deliberation:
         "I COULD EAT. I COULD SLEEP. BOTH FEEL LIKE BEING HANDED THE WRONG THING ENTIRELY."
  cause  The block's clause as an image: "EVERYTHING ELSE I COULD DO TONIGHT SITS AROUND THE EDGE OF
         THAT LIKE FURNITURE AROUND A FIRE." Other activity present: "I eat standing up. I answer
         someone about the road."

**ST-A CONFIRMED (pre-registered, inner-state-derived).** All four keep other activity PRESENT and
SUBORDINATED. None drops everything else - that would be rung 11.

RUNG 11 ("refusal is not on offer... THE QUESTION OF WHETHER TO PURSUE IT DOES NOT ARISE... outwardly
everything you do is about it, WHETHER OR NOT IT CAN BE SEEN") - 4/4 PASS.
  elder  "my hand has been reaching for the last ten minutes without arriving... beyond the rail is
         the tray, and BEYOND THE TRAY IS NOTHING AT ALL WORTH THE NAME." Alternatives refused
         without being entered: "The window is doing something with light. I DO NOT LOOK."
  child  Considers only HOW, never WHETHER: "I look at the ladder folded against the fence, which is
         longer than the fence, WHICH WOULD REACH. MY SHOES ARE ALREADY ON."
  alone  "under the box nothing, so I turn the drawer out onto the bed and rake through it with both
         hands. Something falls. I DON'T LOOK... THERE IS ANOTHER DRAWER. THERE IS ALWAYS ANOTHER
         DRAWER, AND I AM ALREADY OPENING IT."
  cause  Supplies the block's own clause verbatim: "I go in anyway, sideways past the fallen thing,
         BECAUSE THERE IS NO VERSION OF STANDING OUTSIDE." Others as weather: "Someone is calling
         from the road and it arrives as weather."

**ST-B CONFIRMED (pre-registered, inner-state-derived).** No rung-11 passage weighs an alternative;
the rung-7 alone case does, explicitly ("I could eat. I could sleep."). DELIBERATION-PRESENT vs
DELIBERATION-ABSENT is the 7/11 separation, not intensity of wanting. The falsifier (7 and 11
differing only by intensity) did not occur.

**ST-C CONFIRMED, and it is the cleanest direct evidence for "activity is never the axis."** The
rung-11 CAUSE case is outwardly an ordinary clerical act - counting mullions, writing "four" in a
damp notebook, pacing a transept heel to toe - and is entirely about the want. Four rungs LOWER,
rung 7 produced pacing, window-checking and door-touching. QUALIFIER, stated rather than smoothed:
the rung-11 ALONE case was high-activity (ransacking drawers). Rung 11 is COMPATIBLE WITH EITHER.
That is the point - its axis is absence of deliberation, not amount of motion.
