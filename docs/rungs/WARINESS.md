# WARINESS — the thirteen rung blocks

> **AXIS:** how much of you a possible harm has taken over.
> The currency is a SEQUENCE: attention → tone → thought → body → the future → conduct → choice.
> A rung names WHICH FACULTY or capacity has been commandeered by anticipated harm — never raw sympathetic arousal.
> **NOT AROUSAL.** An arousal axis misorders, ranking a clear-headed alarmed person above someone in dread.
> **STATUS 2026-09-07:** authored this session, universalized 13-rung implementation matching `docs/emotion-paths.md` §4. Zero smuggled monsters, weapons, or brawler postures (holds for bedridden elder, child, solitary). All 13 blocks tested clean against own-name, path-name, and primary-name leaks.

## What the sequence axis guarantees

A rung is not an amount of felt adrenaline. It is **how much of your agency and personhood the threat has commandeered**, progressing strictly through the sequence defined in `docs/emotion-paths.md` §4:

```
attention → tone → thought → body → the future → conduct → choice
```

| # | rung | currency / faculty commandeered | state |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | wariness | attention | a sliver of attention reserved |
| 2 | unease | tone | a discordant tone on the situation |
| 3 | misgiving | thought (doubt) | a specific doubt you cannot put down |
| 4 | apprehension | thought (contingency) | active construction of the coming adverse turn |
| 5 | worry | thought (working memory) | thought that returns on its own momentum |
| 6 | nervousness | body (motor excess) | strain crosses into physiology; tension leaks |
| 7 | anxiety | body + thought (sustained) | continuous, unrelenting anticipatory siege |
| 8 | foreboding | the future (conviction) | conviction the blow is coming; preparing to absorb |
| 9 | dread | the future (totalized) | all forward paths lead into ruin |
| 10 | alarm | conduct (interruption) | ongoing task violently broken and abandoned |
| 11 | fright | control of the body | autonomic startle/flinch without conscious evaluation |
| 12 | panic | selection | actions fire without deliberation or comparison |
| 13 | terror | the last of it | conscious agency submerged; organism engulfed |

## The blocks

### 1 — wariness

A small portion of your attention is held back from what you are doing and kept on watch. Nothing is actively going wrong and baseline safety remains in place, but you are no longer operating on complete trust. Outwardly, your pace and posture do not alter, and nobody looking at you would notice anything has changed. What ends this is the margin proving unnecessary.

- **The Sensation.** Your breath is even, your pulse is calm, and only the corners of your perception remain lightly engaged.
- **The Belief.** Things are proceeding as expected, but it costs nothing to keep an eye open. *It is sensible to keep something held in reserve.*
- **The Impulse.** To continue with your work while keeping a fraction of your attention uncommitted.

### 2 — unease

A discordant note has entered your assessment of the situation. It has not attached to a definite fault or named hazard, but the comfortable neutrality of the room is gone. You continue what you were doing, yet every detail now carries a slight question mark beside it. What ends this is an unambiguous sign that everything is in order.

- **The Sensation.** A faint, intermittent coolness at the back of the neck and a stillness in the chest that lingers after you exhale.
- **The Belief.** Something here does not settle cleanly, even if I cannot yet point to it. *The ground is not quite as solid as it appeared.*
- **The Impulse.** To look twice at whatever passes before you, without interrupting your outward routine.

### 3 — misgiving

A specific doubt has formed and will not dissolve under reassurances. You can see the point where the arrangement could fail, and setting the doubt aside requires an active effort of will that does not hold. Observers see you pausing or re-reading, but take you merely for someone being thorough. What ends this is inspecting the critical point and finding it sound.

- **The Sensation.** A persistent knot just beneath the ribs that tightens whenever your attention drifts toward the decision.
- **The Belief.** This arrangement carries a flaw that could turn against me. *I cannot simply trust this to turn out well.*
- **The Impulse.** To test the weak spot, check the seams, and hesitate before committing further.

### 4 — apprehension

The possibility of an adverse turn has moved from a passing question into the center of your thinking. You find yourself constructing the sequence of how the trouble will unfold and what it will cost you if it lands. You are still performing your task, but your thoughts are increasingly occupied with hedging against the blow. What ends this is the source of danger being definitively averted or resolved.

- **The Sensation.** Shallow respiration, a constant dryness on the tongue, and a cold tightness settling across the brow.
- **The Belief.** An unfavourable turn is likely forming, and I will have to reckon with it soon. *I need to prepare for the landing.*
- **The Impulse.** To construct contingencies, limit your commitments, and minimize your exposure before the turn arrives.

### 5 — worry

The prospect of trouble no longer waits for an invitation; it returns to your working memory on its own momentum every time you look away. You cannot keep your mind cleared for other tasks for more than a few moments before the liability reasserts itself. Outwardly, your expressions turn preoccupied and your answers are slightly delayed. What ends this is receiving conclusive proof that the threat has dissipated.

- **The Sensation.** A dull, churning tension in the midsection and a repetitive twitch of alertness that resets without relief.
- **The Belief.** Leaving this unattended leaves me vulnerable, and looking elsewhere does not make it safer. *It will not leave me alone.*
- **The Impulse.** To circle the problem repeatedly, turning every angle over to find a way to make it safe.

### 6 — nervousness

The strain has crossed from your thinking into your physiology. Your body prepares for sudden action even while you attempt to remain still; muscles hold excess tension, breathing pattern is uneven, and small, involuntary movements begin to show in your fingers or feet. Anyone paying close attention can see that your composure is costing you effort to maintain. What ends this is the immediate tension breaking into unambiguous movement.

- **The Sensation.** Tremor at the edges of the hands, a flutter in the stomach, and a cold sweat lining the palms.
- **The Belief.** The situation is unstable and could break open at any second. *My body wants to jump before I tell it to.*
- **The Impulse.** To shift position, fidget, discharge excess tension through minor motions, and brace for an abrupt shift.

### 7 — anxiety

Both your body and your thoughts are locked in a sustained, unrelenting siege of anticipation. The tension does not dip between thoughts; the physiology stays keyed high, draining your reserves and making ordinary concentration exhausting. You are caught in a continuous vigil against an impending crisis that offers no window of relief. What ends this is the situation resolving or exhaustion forcing a collapse of alertness.

- **The Sensation.** A tight band across the chest that restricts full breaths, persistent coldness in the hands, and an elevated, thumping pulse.
- **The Belief.** Something ruinous is hanging over me continuously, and there is no safe place to stand down. *I cannot let my guard drop for an instant.*
- **The Impulse.** To scan restlessly for any sign of escalation, maintain total vigilance, and resist any invitation to relax.

### 8 — foreboding

Doubt and probability have given way to a heavy certainty: the blow is coming, and it will be severe. You have stopped weighing whether things will go wrong and have begun acting on the conviction that disaster is already on its way. Your movements become stiff, quiet, and deliberate, stripped of incidental leisure. What ends this is the anticipated blow striking or an undeniable contradiction of the premise.

- **The Sensation.** A heavy, leaden sink in the guts, a chilling of the skin, and a quiet slowing of breath under overwhelming pressure.
- **The Belief.** There is no longer any question of avoiding it; the ruin is on its way. *It is coming, and it will not miss.*
- **The Impulse.** To batten down whatever can be protected, withdraw from vulnerable margins, and wait for the impact.

### 9 — dread

The impending harm has expanded to swallow the horizon; you can no longer conceive of an outcome where you emerge whole. The future presents itself not as a field of possibilities, but as a narrow corridor leading into inevitable ruin. Outwardly, your affect is drained, your gaze fixed, and initiative for any long-term project drops to zero. What ends this is the blow finally landing and the period of waiting ending.

- **The Sensation.** A crushing cold in the chest cavity, limbs that feel heavy and unresponsive, and a dry, suffocating constriction in the throat.
- **The Belief.** The catastrophe has already claimed the outcome; there is nowhere ahead that is safe. *Everything forward leads into the blow.*
- **The Impulse.** To freeze in place, pull your limbs inward, and endure the interval until the impact hits.

### 10 — alarm

An immediate, undeniable warning arrives and violently breaks your ongoing conduct. Whatever you were saying, writing, or holding is instantly abandoned; the hazard demands total and immediate redirection of your faculties. Outwardly, you freeze mid-sentence or drop your current task with sudden, unambiguous urgency that arrests everyone around you. What ends this is determining that the immediate warning was an error or neutralizing the point of contact.

- **The Sensation.** A sharp, icy jolt through the spine, instantaneous dilation of the eyes, and a sharp, audible gasp that arrests the breath.
- **The Belief.** The danger is here now, and continuing what I was doing is impossible. *Drop everything and look.*
- **The Impulse.** To halt ongoing activity instantly, snap all focus directly to the hazard, and position for immediate defense.

### 11 — fright

The threat is immediately upon you and command over your own body is seized by autonomic recoil. The decision to flinch, cower, or recoil is not evaluated; the body pulls back from the hazard before conscious reasoning can intervene. Outwardly, you jump back, shield your vital centers, or shudder with stark physical revulsion. What ends this is the immediate physical proximity breaking or barriers intervening.

- **The Sensation.** A violent lurch in the diaphragm, a flood of icy blood to the core, and an involuntary shudder racking the shoulders.
- **The Belief.** It is right on top of me and I am unprotected. *Pull back before it hits!*
- **The Impulse.** To flinch away, shield vulnerable areas with whatever is at hand, and pull back with desperate autonomic recoil.

### 12 — panic

Deliberate selection of action has broken down completely. The capacity to compare alternatives, weigh consequences, or evaluate trade-offs is gone; the organism executes frantic, uncalculated escape or thrashing without deliberation. Outwardly, your actions become erratic, disorganized, and desperate, knocking aside obstacles without regard for dignity or cost. What ends this is reaching physical safety or being forcefully restrained and grounded.

- **The Sensation.** Suffocating shortness of breath, blood roaring in the ears, dizzying vertigo, and a desperate, burning compulsion to move.
- **The Belief.** Staying here is immediate destruction; there is no time to choose how. *Out, out, get out now!*
- **The Impulse.** To scramble, thrash, shove past any impediment, and break from the space with blind, disorganized desperation.

### 13 — terror

The last reserves of conscious agency are submerged by overwhelming, catastrophic extremity. You are completely engulfed; the boundary between yourself and the destroying reality has broken down, leaving only the raw, helpless agony of an organism facing obliteration. Outwardly, you are either locked in rigid, voiceless petrification or crying out in primal, unmodulated extremity. What ends this is surviving the climax of the event, physical exhaustion, or the extinction of consciousness.

- **The Sensation.** Total sensory overload followed by tunneling darkness, icy paralysis through every limb, and complete numbness to pain.
- **The Belief.** Destruction is total, absolute, and already here; nothing remains to be saved. *It is the end of everything.*
- **The Impulse.** To collapse into total immobility, curl into the smallest possible space, or give way against the crushing darkness.

## The descent blocks

**The way down is not the climb reversed** (`docs/emotion-dynamics.md`, "The descent words are a
separate list"; the rules are in `DISPLEASURE.md` under the same heading). The pivot is **rung 9,
dread**: the last rung on which what is taken is the future rather than the body — from alarm up, the
sequence commandeers conduct, control, selection and the last of agency, and a body that has been
seized is not un-seized by the harm receding. Above the pivot each rung carries a second block for the
mood that is no longer being fed: the hazard is not being re-read, and the organism is still where the
fear left it. At and below dread, the climb block serves both directions.

### 10 — alarm

**[State: alarm]**
The warning has been looked at, and what you dropped is still where it fell. The redirection completed and nothing has taken its place; your faculties are pointed at where the hazard was and are not returning to what they were doing. Outwardly you are stopped mid-task with the task abandoned around you, and no new one has begun. What ends this is picking the dropped thing back up, which the body resists for a while before it allows.

- **The Sensation.** The jolt gone and a hollow readiness in its place. Hands empty. The eyes still wide.
- **The Belief.** I stopped everything for it, and I have not started anything since. *It was there.*
- **The Impulse.** To look again at where the hazard was, rather than back at what you were doing.

### 11 — fright

**[State: fright]**
The recoil has completed and the body is still braced in it. The flinch is held past the thing that caused it; the shield is up and nothing is pressing on it. Outwardly you are pulled back and covering, and you are not coming out. What ends this is the muscles releasing on their own — minutes, not seconds — and they do not take instruction.

- **The Sensation.** Aftershocks of the shudder through the shoulders. The cold in the core ebbing. A brace that will not drop when told to.
- **The Belief.** It did not hit, and the body has not been told. *Not yet.*
- **The Impulse.** To keep whatever was shielding you where it is, and to check that the thing is not still there.

### 12 — panic

**[State: panic]**
The scramble has stopped without deliberate selection coming back. You are wherever the last movement left you, and you do not know the sequence that put you there; what was knocked aside stays where it went. Outwardly you are still, and the stillness is not chosen either. What ends this is breath returning under your own count.

- **The Sensation.** Breath still short without the compulsion behind it. Dizziness. Legs that will not take a plan.
- **The Belief.** I stopped. I do not know what I did to get here. *Here.*
- **The Impulse.** Not to move until moving is chosen again, and it is not yet.

### 13 — terror

**[State: terror]**
The engulfment is not being renewed. What remains is the organism the event went through: the boundary between you and it is re-forming, and what it encloses is shaking. There is a you again and it is very small. Outwardly you are locked in the posture the extremity left you in, and sound, if any, has gone out of you. What ends this is the body's exhaustion passing into sleep, or being held still by someone until it does.

- **The Sensation.** The numbness giving way to cold. Hearing returning as a ring. Tremor in every limb, and no strength to stop it.
- **The Belief.** It happened, and there is still someone here; that is as far as it goes. *Still here.*
- **The Impulse.** To stay in the smallest space you can make, and not to be seen.
