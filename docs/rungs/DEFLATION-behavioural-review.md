## WHY THIS PATH NEXT, AND WHAT IT TESTS BEYOND ITSELF

RECEPTIVITY needed TWO graded variables, hinging at rung 8: below it, what has been TAKEN from you
without your deciding; above it, what COMMAND you have left. A single variable across the whole
ladder mis-scores - it is what makes rung 5 (functional, higher) and rung 9 (loud, lower) look
misplaced when they are not.

DEFLATION's design already names a hinge at 0.60 (docs/rungs/DEFLATION.md:3) and says in as many
words that its halves want different questions: "the scope half wants its own question - HOW MUCH OF
THEIR LIFE IS COLOURED BY IT - and the faculties half keeps CAN STILL WANT / HAS A FUTURE."
That was written as a note for a future pass and never tested. Testing it here decides whether
"a hinged ladder needs two graded variables" is a fact about ladder design or a quirk of one path.
