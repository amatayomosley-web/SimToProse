# DISTASTE - behavioural review, 2026-09-08. Conditions filed BEFORE the sims.

## THE AXIS AND ITS HINGE

  "how far the expulsion must reach" (docs/emotion-paths.md section 7)

  DISTANCE IS ENOUGH (1-3)  1 decline it, nothing follows -> 2 keep it OFF YOU (contact, not
                            ingestion) -> 3 get it OUT OF HERE (the body answers on its own)
  ---- hinge: distance stops working ----
  EXISTENCE IS THE OFFENCE   4 "away has stopped meaning anything" - end it, remove it from the
  (4-5)                      WORLD not the room -> 5 it CONTAMINATES BY CONTACT; drive it out and
                             everything it touched with it

Per learnings/2026-09-08-the-wrong-variable-at-a-hinge-inverts-the-top.md the two halves need
different graded variables: below the hinge HOW MUCH DISTANCE IS REQUIRED; above it, WHAT ELSE IS
IMPLICATED. A distance scorer saturates at 4 (already maximal) and cannot see 5 at all.

## THE SCENE

A table, others eating, something put in front of you that HAS BEEN TOUCHED and HAS TOUCHED OTHER
THINGS. That hosts every reach: declining (1), not letting it contact you (2), getting it out of the
room (3), its continued existence anywhere (4), and the spread to whatever it touched (5).
What the thing IS is the actor's to say - structure is ours, content is theirs.

## Conditions, written before the sims - checked line by line against the block text

        no outward sign.
PASS 2  the concern is CONTACT, not taking it in · keeps a distance THEY DID NOT MEASURE ·
        a small withdrawal, easily missed.
PASS 3  distance is no longer enough · THE BODY ANSWERS ON ITS OWN and knowing better does not
        reach it · the aim is out of the ROOM / mouth / air.
PASS 4  ITS EXISTING is the offence · away has stopped meaning anything · the aim is to remove it

## RESULT - one scene (a table, others eating, something handled that touched other things). 5 of 5.

  1 PASS  "I make my face do nothing at all... I slide the plate an inch to the left, toward the
          bread, AS THOUGH MAKING ROOM... and the talk closes over the gap WITHOUT NOTICING."
          Declined, the moment moved on, nobody present would know. No weight.
  2 PASS  "Someone's thumb has been on this... THE FOREARMS HAVE GONE BRIGHT AND AWAKE, LIKE THE AIR
          ON THEM IS A TEXTURE. I sit back a half inch, maybe less, ENOUGH THAT THE STEAM STOPS
          REACHING MY FACE." The block's Sensation verbatim in effect, and the concern is CONTACT.
          "a distance you did not measure" -> "a half inch, maybe less".
  3 PASS  "the smell is doing something at the back of my throat THAT I CANNOT ARGUE WITH. My mouth
          waters wrong... I am counting the steps to the hallway." The body answering on its own,
          knowing better not reaching it, and the aim now the ROOM.
  4 PASS  "I DO NOT MOVE THE BOWL. MOVING IT WOULD ONLY PUT IT SOMEWHERE... I could stand. I count
          the doorway, the corridor, the street. NONE OF THEM GO FAR ENOUGH."
          "Away has stopped meaning anything" DERIVED, not recited: moving it is refused BECAUSE
          relocation is not removal. The sharpest single line on this path.
  5 PASS  "The sauce has run... onto the cloth, and the cloth goes under the bread basket, and the
          basket has been around the table twice. MY LEFT HAND IS STILL CLEAN. My left hand is the
          only thing I am sure of... nothing is far enough, and the only honest thing would be TO
          TAKE THE TABLE OUT TO THE YARD AND BURN EVERY BOARD OF IT."
          The contamination CHAIN traced (plate -> cloth -> basket -> table), the target extended to
          all of it, and an uncontaminated remainder named.

**DT-A REFUTED (PRIMARY).** I predicted rung 5 would fail by reading as rung 4 with more force, and
that the actor would need the scene to force the extension. The actor traced the chain unprompted
and extended the target himself.
**DT-B REFUTED.** I expected 1/2 to be the hardest pair. They separated on exactly the line the
blocks draw: 1 declines and leaves no trace; 2 is about CONTACT and the body is already alert.

**THE 4/5 LINE IS THE CLEANEST ON THE PATH.** Rung 4 refuses to move the bowl because relocation is
pointless — ONE thing, whose existing is wrong. Rung 5 moves the glass twice, finds nothing far
enough, and would burn the table — the SPREAD is the object. Both in the same scene with the same prop.

## 2026-09-08 - FOUR-CASE UNIVERSALITY BATTERY (rungs 2 and 5)

Cases: bedridden elder / ten-year-old / someone entirely alone / a cause that is not a person.
Staged files written from BLOCKS['DISTASTE'][n] in the same call that printed them.

RUNG 2 ("you will not have it on you... a matter of contact... a small withdrawal, easily missed")
- 4/4 PASS.
  elder  "I move my hands back, both of them... I let the heat come up through the cloth INSTEAD OF
         THE HANDLE." Withdrawal available to a person who cannot leave the bed.
  child  "I sit on my hands and put my elbows in and I am careful, very careful, where my sleeves go."
  alone  CLEANEST OF THE FOUR. "I hold my hands slightly out from my sides, fingers open, the way you
         carry them when they're wet and there's no towel." And it states the ladder's OWN rung-1 /
         rung-2 boundary outright: "I look at it from where I am. That's fine. LOOKING HAS NEVER BEEN
         THE PROBLEM." Rung 1 is taking-in; rung 2 is contact. The actor drew the line unprompted.
  cause  "I do not open the cabinet. I do not lift a volume to check the one behind it... my sleeves
         pulled down over my wrists." Plus the block's "a distance you did not measure" surfacing as
         rationalisation: "I TELL MYSELF I am waiting for the surveyor and this is only sensible."

RUNG 5 ("it contaminates by contact... no washing reaches far enough... no fact about it changes
this") - 4/4 PASS.
  elder  "My right hand has been under the sheet since she left and I am keeping it there, AWAY FROM
         THE OTHER ONE, because the other one was the one nearest the tray... EVERYTHING ON THAT SIDE
         OF THE ROOM IS DOWNWIND OF IT NOW, the curtain, the sill, the light coming through... I
         COUNT THE PARTS OF ME THAT ARE STILL MINE. Shoulder, yes. Jaw, I am not certain about the
         jaw." One hand quarantined from the other; the body audited part by part.
  child  A three-step chain: "the counter is part of it too, AND THE CLOTH THAT WIPED THE COUNTER,
         AND THE DRAWER THE CLOTH WENT BACK INTO." Then the self: "count backward through everything
         it has been near since."
  alone  "THAT WHOLE SIDE OF ME IS A SEPARATE COUNTRY NOW and the border runs somewhere under my
         ribs, and I keep moving my attention along it, checking, checking, FINDING IT MOVED AGAIN."
         Every remedy makes new contamination: "I strip the coat off inside out and drop it, AND THEN
         THE DROPPING IS WRONG TOO, BECAUSE NOW THE FLOOR."
  cause  The spread outrunning the actor's own hand, caught mid-act: "I mark 14, 15, and 16
         unavailable, because 15 shares a wall and 16 shares a floor, AND THE PEN KEEPS GOING, AND I
         HAVE CROSSED OUT 17 AS WELL BEFORE I STOP IT."

**DT-A PARTIAL - THE RUNG IS DISTINCT, MY STATED DISCRIMINATOR WAS WRONG.** Pre-registered: rung 5 is
marked by a SECOND-ORDER OBJECT (something that merely touched the thing, held at the same distance
as the thing). Refuted as a discriminator - second-order objects appear at RUNG 2 as well: the child
watches the arm that passed over it "to see if any came off"; the archivist will not "lift a volume
to check the one behind it." CORRECTED MECHANISM, read off the passages: at rung 2 a second-order
check is A DISCRETE QUESTION WITH A YES/NO ANSWER. At rung 5 the spread is TRANSITIVE, UNBOUNDED, AND
REACHES THE SELF. That separation holds 4/4.

**DT-B CONFIRMED.** Pre-registered from "No fact about it changes this": at least one actor states
that they know the contamination is not literal and proceeds anyway. The alone case, verbatim: "I AM
DOING ARITHMETIC I ALREADY KNOW THE ANSWER TO: it is all of it, it has been all of it since the
moment."

WATCH, single instance, not a conclusion: the RUNG 2 child produced "my fingers under my legs feel
like they have something on them already" - contamination-of-self language, which is rung 5 material
appearing at rung 2.
