## RUNGS 1-7 — THE IDEA, and why the ceiling's variable does not work down here

Residual command is the right variable from rung 8 up, where something is being LOST. It is useless
at 1-5: a person at rung 5 has full command, they are simply lighter. The variable that runs the
lower ladder is WHAT HAS BEEN TAKEN WITHOUT YOUR DECIDING IT.

   1  NOTHING YET      "You are not closed, and you did not open... nothing has yet." Impulse: none.
   2  YOUR LEANING     "Something has your leaning, AND YOU DID NOT DECIDE TO LEAN."

   4  YOUR BODY        "Your body took it up BEFORE YOU HAD ANY VIEW ABOUT IT... a softening you did

   6  YOUR CATEGORIES  "your mind pauses trying to match it... I do not have a measure for this."
