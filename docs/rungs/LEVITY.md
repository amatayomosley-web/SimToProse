## What was measured

**THE ORDER SORTS AS TEXT — for the wrong reason.** Three sorters, mean 0.978. Asked what they ranked
on, all three named the axis correctly (*"how much choosing-authority has been surrendered"*). But
their reversal notes show *how* they recovered 1–5: *"I sequenced id5 after id4 because id5's language
textually presupposes id4's frame."* The low rungs chain by dependency — 5 presupposes 4's frame, 3
presupposes 2's letting-through — and sorters followed the chain. **rho 0.978 measured that the blocks
link, not that they climb one quantity.**

**IN PERFORMANCE, RUNG 5 IS THE FLOOR. THREE FOR THREE.**

| scenario | recovered (true 2 → 5 → 8) | rung 5 depth |
|---|---|---|
| bedridden woman, crossword, alone | 5, 2, 8 | **2** |
| boy and grandfather, potatoes | 5, 2 (rung 8 actor failed schema) | **2** |
| stairwell, partner stopped laughing | 5, 8, 2 | **2** |

The judges' reasons are identical and correct on the stated axis. Crossword: *"every micro-movement
explicitly checked before the next is taken… she is still narrating and deciding each inch."* Potatoes:
*"pre-stages the next potato… a calibrated test… judgement visibly runs before each escalation."*
Stairwell: *"chooser fully present and steering throughout — 'he decided to let it', explicit gating of
an initial reflex."* **A character who hunts openings and tests whether the frame holds is the most
steering character on the ladder.** On an axis of *choosing handed over*, that is the floor.

**The not-disinhibition rule held where the design draws it and failed where the design abandons it.**
At rung 5 the check *ran and passed* in all three passages — selective, inside the frame, no cruelty.
At rung 8 the stairwell judge wrote: *"Nothing in him was checking the landing, or measuring how long
this had run… judgement fails to run rather than running and passing — disinhibited."* A lens read
block 6 — *"looking has stopped happening"* — as *"disinhibition's actual mechanism."* Both are reading
the design's own words for rungs 6 and up.

**The free scene beat did not fire.** All three stairwell performers noticed the partner had stopped;
5 and 8 kept going anyway. The rung-2 actor played the *listener* rather than the performer — the
scenario let them choose, which is my defect, not the design's.

**The empty room broke rungs 4–5 exactly as predicted, and the actor's workaround is the proof.** Alone
with a crossword, told to push *"to see whether the frame holds,"* the rung-5 actor used **the
bedframe** — and had a woman who cannot get up test whether the floor would take her weight. Their
friction note: *"the line about a frame holding maps oddly onto a body and a bed… I kept the testing
literal."* The design says levity has no object; rungs 4–5 require a second mind.

## The finding: two axes glued at 5/6

The stated axis is *how much of your choosing you have handed to what is available.*

**Rungs 6–9 are on it.** Checking stops (6), pace is ceded (7), choice is ceded (8), no chooser (9).
Judges placed rung 8 at depths 8, 7, 7 — where it belongs.

**Rungs 1–5 are not.** 1 notices an opening and hands over nothing. 2 lets one through — a choice. 3 is
disposed to take the next — readiness to choose. 4 is inside the frame with *the check still running* —
choosing, inside a licence. 5 hunts — maximum choosing. **Agency rises from 1 to 5 and falls from 6 to
9.** That is two quantities with a hinge between them: the DEFLATION hinge (hurt below, capacity above)
and DISPLEASURE's original fork, and the reason performance inverts while text sorts.

**The design's own 0.55 anchor disagrees with its own rung word.** The engine anchor at 0.55 is *"you
would keep going past usefulness"* — a can't-stop, which is on the surrender axis. The rung at
0.50–0.62 is named *mischief — you HUNT openings*, which is on the engagement axis. The anchor and the
word describe different people at the same number.

This is not one rung against its axis, as RECEPTIVITY's elation and SELF-REGARD's vanity were. It is
half a ladder. Compiling it would hand `rung_at(0.55)` a block that three judges read as the floor,
labelled as mid-surrender — a known inversion in the engine.

## 2026-09-08 — TWO PURPOSE CLAIMS TESTED. Both hold. The fork is unchanged.

Fourteen blind actors, PASS/FAIL pre-registered before any sim ran. This tests **why the path should
exist**, not whether its ladder climbs — the 0-of-3 inversion above is untouched and still blocks
compilation. Transcripts and full scoring: the session scratchpad, `lv/PREREG.md`,
`lv/results_test1.md`, `lv/results_test2.md`.

### 1. LEVITY IS NOT REDUNDANT — passed at 0 of 6

Design claim: *"it is therefore the only path that argues for a NON-OPTIMAL action — without it every
character is an optimiser, and optimisers are not people."* Falsifiable form: if a built path produces a
non-instrumental act as readily, the claim is not LEVITY's to carry.

Two scenes (a task with one other person), four conditions, N=2.

| condition | non-instrumental act |
|---|---|
| **LEVITY rung 4** | **2 of 2** |
| GOODWILL rung 5 | 0 of 2 |
| STIRRING rung 5 | 0 of 2 |
| no block at all | 0 of 2 |

The unblocked control produced the predicted optimiser on demand: the two finish the count, agree the
number, and the partner is already reaching for the next load — not one act that was not the job. The
LEVITY actor's character instead **did not want to be called away yet**:
the character now prefers the opening to the task, which an optimiser cannot write.

A contrast nobody designed for: the STIRRING actor also used the word OPENING — *"I hear it as an
opening"* — and took it as a chance to slip out a side door. Same word, opposite path. Wanting reads an
opening as an exit; levity reads it as an invitation.

**Not shown:** only two of the seven other built paths were run as comparisons, and **RECEPTIVITY was
not among them** — it is the near-neighbour most likely to produce purposeless behaviour and remains
untested against this claim.

### 2. RE-PRICING SURVIVES AS BLOCK PROSE — and refutes what I told the owner

Design claim: *"inside the frame an attempt is deniable... so a character acts on their stirring,
displeasure or goodwill at LOWER magnitude than they otherwise would."*

Affect held at DISPLEASURE rung 5 in both cells; the LEVITY 4 block is the only variable. Three scenes
in which the character has a grievance they have not raised.

| cell | delivered | withheld | cruel |
|---|---|---|---|
| DISPLEASURE 5 alone | 0 of 3 | **3 of 3** | 0 |
| DISPLEASURE 5 + LEVITY 4 | **3 of 3** | 0 | **0 of 3** |

Same scene, same affect, with and without the frame. **Without:** *"I mean to ask him about the fence
line, about the price of feed, anything ordinary, but the words won't come... I straighten another crate
that doesn't need straightening."* **With:** *"I laugh too, quicker than I'd answer plainly, and say it
might carry lighter in last year's harness... **out past this yard, either line lands hard. Here,
laughing, it slides through clean**, and he knows it the same as I do."*

The mechanism is stated in the actor's own words in 2 of 3. Nothing landed as cruelty — the
disinhibition failure this file warns about did not appear.

**I had claimed compiling the ladder could not deliver this**, because a block's whole fate is
`src/engine/prompt.py:78` — prose in the staging slot — and no threshold machinery exists for it to
modify. The machinery genuinely does not exist; the inference from that was wrong. The actor is a
language model, so handed the frame as prose it prices the act itself. **In this engine a mechanism can
be carried entirely by block prose with no engine-side implementation.**

**Against my own PASS:** the block states the rule outright (*"In here it does not land the way it would
out there"*), so the actors were told it and restated it — this is compliance, not emergence, and should
not be reported as emergence. It stands anyway, because the question was whether the composed prompt
produces re-priced BEHAVIOUR, and the behavioural gap is 3 of 3 against 0 of 3. Second, weaker: cell F
receives more text than cell E, and a length-matched control (DISPLEASURE 5 + an unrelated path's block)
was not run.

### 3. A THIRD FINDING, unlooked-for: the frame defect reproduced from a new direction

The meeting-room actor delivered the barb and wrote *"nobody else is here yet to soften what I say, and
we both know it, so I don't take it back"* — the deniability explicitly ABSENT, the barb delivered
anyway. It took the block's DELIVERY (aimed, faster than ordinary speech) without the block's MECHANISM.

This is the secondary defect already recorded above — *rungs 4-5 need a partner, which the design's "no
object" claim does not allow for* — arrived at independently. Two of three scenes supplied a shared
frame and the mechanism appeared; the two-person room with a live grievance did not, and it didn't. The
block's own exit condition (*"the other one stepping out of it"*) presupposes a frame that a scene is
not guaranteed to provide, and nothing checks whether one exists.

### WHAT THIS CHANGES, AND WHAT IT DOES NOT

**Changed:** the path's PURPOSE is no longer asserted, it is measured. Both claims the design makes for
LEVITY's existence held under blind test, and the "just build it as flavour" reading is dead — the
re-pricing does real work on the other paths' willingness to act.

**Unchanged: the fork below is still the owner's, and none of this decides it.** (a), (b) and (c) are
arguments about what the ladder MEASURES; these tests exercised rung 4 alone and say nothing about
order. Rung 5 is still at the floor 3 for 3, and that is still what blocks compilation.

**New, and bearing on the fork:** whichever option is taken, rungs 4-5 need a scene condition — a shared
frame — that no other path requires and nothing currently supplies or checks.

## 2026-09-09 — IS LEVITY A PATH, OR A MODIFIER ON THE OTHER EIGHT?

Four more blind actors, run to falsify a reading of the passages above rather than to confirm it.
Eighteen actors total across the battery. Full record: session scratchpad `lv/results_test3.md`.

**The reading:** LEVITY's own contribution is a STRUCTURE, not a subject — speech AIMED, ACCELERATED,
inside a BOUNDED SPACE that re-prices what is said, which the character WANTS HELD OPEN. Those four
appear in every passage the block produced. What differs between passages is only the content poured
into them, and that content comes from whatever ELSE is on the packet.

| loaded with LEVITY 4 | scene | reads as | frame held open? |
|---|---|---|---|
| nothing | two adults | courtship | yes |
| nothing | boy + grandfather | a shared bit with rules | yes |
| nothing | **nobody present** | **rumination** | fabricated |
| DISPLEASURE 5 | three scenes | a duel | yes |
| WARINESS 5 | grain | banter as escape | yes |
| GOODWILL 5 | grain | teasing that yields | **CLOSED** |

The design already says this in part — *"it is the only path with no object... not ABOUT anything"* —
and §9 names courtship, challenge and testing loyalty as things that run THROUGH it. What the battery
adds is the consequence: **having no object of its own, it takes its object from the co-loaded path.**

### Three findings that bear on the fork

**1. The courtship in the two-adult passages was the SCENE, not the path.** Put the same block in a
ten-year-old peeling potatoes with his grandfather and there is no romance and no reach for any —
instead, the grandfather's customs-officer voice, played straight: *"not smiling, because smiling
breaks it. Out in the regular kitchen that voice would just be silly. In here it's a real country with
real laws."* That is the re-pricing rule stated more plainly than any adult actor managed.

**2. GOODWILL BEATS LEVITY, and it is the only thing that did.** In nine passages only one character
closes the frame: *"I make myself slow down and recount the last row aloud, needing the number more
than I need this to keep going."* Care for the shared work — which is care for her — overrode the
block's own keep-it-open impulse. So the container is not neutral, and a co-loaded path can win
against it. Worth knowing before deciding what LEVITY is.

**3. THE EMPTY ROOM DID NOT BREAK IT — IT FABRICATED A PARTNER, and that is worse.** This file
predicted a break, and recorded a prior rung-5 actor working around it with an OBJECT (a bedframe).
The new actor, alone by lamplight, invented an ABSENT PERSON and ran the frame against him: *"the rest
of me keeps turning his last words over, answering them again, better this time, in the quiet he can't
get into... I can say the truer, sharper version of everything, AND WE'VE BOTH AGREED IT WON'T COUNT."*

Both halves matter. The genre is RUMINATION — rehearsing an argument, which nothing named LEVITY should
emit at rung 4 with no other state loaded; note the actor supplied the grievance with no DISPLEASURE
block present, the container reaching for conflict to have any content at all. And *"we've both agreed
it won't count"* is a deniability clause applied to someone who is not there and has agreed to nothing.

Set that beside the meeting-room actor of 2026-09-08, who recorded the deniability as explicitly ABSENT
and delivered the barb anyway. Two different failure shapes, one root: **nothing checks that a shared
frame exists, so the block asserts one into being.** Whichever of (a)/(b)/(c) is chosen, rungs 4-5 need
a scene precondition no other path requires and nothing currently supplies.

### WHAT THIS ADDS TO THE OWNER'S FORK

(a), (b) and (c) all assume LEVITY is a ladder like the other eight. The battery says it behaves like a
MODIFIER — an intensity of FRAME rather than an intensity of feeling. That is a coherent object and it
may well want rungs, but it is a different KIND of object from STIRRING or WARINESS, and the two-axes
problem recorded above may be a symptom of forcing it into the same shape. **A fourth option now has
evidence behind it: LEVITY is not a ninth path but a state that MODIFIES the other eight.** Nothing here
decides it, and rung 5 is still at the floor 3 for 3.

**AGAINST ALL OF THE ABOVE:** N is 1 per cell in this battery, one scene family for the co-loaded pairs,
rung 4 only. The WARINESS actor near-quotes the block (*"in here it doesn't land the way it would out
there"*), so it is weaker evidence than the rest. And the four markers are all stated or implied IN the
block, so their appearance is compliance — what is being measured is which SUBJECT each pairing selects,
not whether the actor invented the structure.

## Rules every block obeys

1. **Structure is ours; content is the actor's.** No block names what the opening was, who is inside
   the frame with you, or what the activity is. The conditional form (*nobody watching would see*)
   is correct; a role-noun is not.
2. **Not disinhibition.** At every rung the check RUNS. Low on the ladder it runs and you act on its
   answer; high on the ladder it runs less and less often, until at the top there is no one left to
   run it. A brake that fails is a different path's business.
3. **No object.** Nothing here is about anything. What is handed over is choosing itself, and the
   blocks say what has taken it — an opening, the frame, the activity, the pace — never what it is for.
4. **Four-case universal.** No standing, leaning, turning, walking. Alone or in company, upright or
   flat, the block holds.
5. **The name is never delivered.**

## The blocks

### 1 — amusement

An opening registers, and it pleases you. That is all: you have handed nothing over, and everything you do is still for something. Outwardly nothing — the corner of the mouth at most, and only if nobody is looking. What would take this away is the next thing needing doing.

- **The Sensation.** A small lift behind the sternum. The face stays where it was.
- **The Belief.** That was a good one. *I saw that.*
- **The Impulse.** None. It is enjoyed and kept.

### 2 — levity

You let one through. It was not for anything and you said it anyway, or did it, and then you went back to what you were doing — but you go back to it, once, and it is still good. Outwardly a remark that could have been left out, or a small unnecessary thing done. What would take this away is the room requiring you to be useful again.

- **The Sensation.** The breath comes out a little easier than it went in.
- **The Belief.** There was room for that. *Why not.*
- **The Impulse.** To let it stand; not to chase it.

### 3 — playfulness

You are disposed toward openings now. You are not looking for them, but when the next one comes you will take it, and you know that about yourself in this moment. The work still gets done, a little slower, a little lighter. Outwardly you are easier to be near than you were. What would take this away is something going wrong that needs the whole of you.

- **The Sensation.** A readiness low in the chest, like waiting for a door to open.
- **The Belief.** This can carry a little more. *Go on, then.*
- **The Impulse.** To take the next opening when it comes, and to make one if it is slow coming.

### 4 — banter

The exchange is inside the frame now, and inside it you say what you would not say outside it. Everything can be taken back — *only joking* — and so you can put more on the table than you could afford to mean, and you do. The check still runs: you know exactly what would land wrong, and you go up to that line and not over. Outwardly quick, close, a little dangerous to anyone who has not been let in. What would take this away is one of you stepping out of the frame; then whatever was last said counts.

- **The Sensation.** Quickness. The next thing is ready before the last has finished.
- **The Belief.** In here it costs nothing. *I can say that.*
- **The Impulse.** To answer at once, and to answer with a little more than was given.

### 5 — mischief

You are hunting openings now, and you push on the ones you find to see whether the frame holds. Every push is a test and you want the test to pass — that is the whole pleasure of it, that it holds. You choose who can take it and what they are safe in, and you would never push where it would break; if it broke, that would be the end of the game, and the game is the point. Outwardly you are a nuisance to exactly the people who can bear it. What would take this away is a push that lands as real.

- **The Sensation.** A brightness in the eyes and hands; the body wants to be doing something a little wrong.
- **The Belief.** It will hold. *Let me see if it holds.*
- **The Impulse.** To press, lightly, at the place that is safest to press.

### 6 — immersion

You have stopped checking outside. Whatever this is, it has the whole of your attention and you did not decide to give it; you notice, when something outside interrupts, that you had not been keeping watch for some time. Outwardly you are hard to reach, and slow to answer when reached. What would take this away is a break in the thing itself, not anything around it.

- **The Sensation.** The edges of the room have gone soft. Sounds arrive from far off.
- **The Belief.** Nothing else is happening. *Just this.*
- **The Impulse.** To stay in, and to resent, mildly, being pulled out.

### 7 — absorption

The activity sets the pace now, not you. You do not decide when the next part comes; it comes, and you are there for it. There is still a you inside this, but it is a passenger — pleased, and not steering. Outwardly whatever the activity looks like from outside, done with a fluency that does not look decided. What would take this away is the activity ending, and for a moment after it ends you will not know what to do with your hands.

- **The Sensation.** The body is doing things ahead of the thought that would have chosen them.
- **The Belief.** It is carrying me. *I am not the one doing this, quite.*
- **The Impulse.** To keep up.

### 8 — raptness

The activity chooses, and you follow. What comes next is not offered to you for approval; it simply comes, and you are already doing it. Time has stopped being counted. Outwardly, if anyone is watching, you look as though you are not there — and in the sense that matters, you are not. What would take this away is nothing you would notice from inside it.

- **The Sensation.** Very little. The body is a thing that happens.
- **The Belief.** There is no question. *Yes.*
- **The Impulse.** Whatever the activity's next step is, already begun.

### 9 — flow

There is no gap between an opening and the act, and no one in the gap to choose. The thing is being done and there is no you standing beside it deciding to. Outwardly the same as the rung below, and only afterward, if it ends, will there have been anyone to say what it was like — and what they will say is that there was no outside to it. What would take this away cannot be said from here; there is nowhere else to say it from.

- **The Sensation.** None reported. Afterward: as if surfacing.
- **The Belief.** None. There is no room for one.
- **The Impulse.** The act is the impulse; they are not two things.

## The descent blocks

**The way down is not the climb reversed** (`docs/emotion-dynamics.md`; the rules are in
`DISPLEASURE.md` under the same heading). The pivot is **rung 7, absorption**: the last rung with a
passenger inside — pleased, not steering, and still someone. Above it the choosing has been handed over
entirely and then the one who could be handed it, and the activity ending does not hand them back at
once; they surface, and the surfacing is its own state (`emotion-paths.md`: "as if surfacing").
Above the pivot each rung carries a second block for the mood that has stopped being fed; at and below
absorption, the climb block serves both directions.

### 8 — raptness

**[State: raptness]**
Time is being counted again, and the count says more went by than you have. What comes next is being offered for approval and you have no opinion about it. Outwardly you are there, if anyone is watching, and not yet doing anything with it. What would take this away is the room arriving — voices, the light, what is owed — and it arrives on its own.

- **The Sensation.** Very little, still. The body is a thing that happened.
- **The Belief.** It let go of me. *Where was I.*
- **The Impulse.** To sit with it. Not to pick anything up yet.

### 9 — flow

**[State: flow]**
There is someone beside the act again, and they arrived late: the thing was done, and now there is a you who was not there for it. The gap between an opening and the act is back, and no one is in it yet. Outwardly you have stopped, and only now is there anyone to say what it was like. What would take this away is the surfacing finishing, minutes, and it leaves you not knowing what to do with your hands.

- **The Sensation.** The body handed back. Hands that were doing something are yours, and idle.
- **The Belief.** That happened, and I was not the one there. *I was in it.*
- **The Impulse.** To go back under. It does not open.
