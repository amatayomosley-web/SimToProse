## What is measured, and what it cost to learn

**THE ORDER WAS EVIDENCED BEFORE AUTHORING.** This path passed a blind sort at mean rho 0.900
(opus 0.950 / fable 0.917 / sonnet 0.833, 7 distinct levels from 11 rungs, no fork) before any block
existed. Two further sorts on the authored spines recovered it at 0.915. All three sorters, asked what
quantity they ranked on, named the given-over stack unprompted and not "how intense it sounds."

**FULL BLOCKS PERFORM WHERE SPINES DO NOT.** Identical scenarios and rungs, the only variable being
block versus one-line spine: spines recovered the performed order in **0 of 3** scenarios and read the
FLOOR rung at depth 8 / 4 / 8; full blocks recovered **1 of 3** exactly and read the floor at 2 / 2 / 6.
The Sensation bullet — *the face at rest, the breath unhurried and not held* — anchors what a spine
cannot. **Author blocks, never spines.** This was a confound in the earlier measurements on this path
and on DEFLATION, and it accounts for part of the text-order / performance-order gap. Not all of it.

**THE AGENCY HOLD IS REAL AND IT BIT.** `emotion-paths.md` records that the character *"must not
deliberately arrange herself at any rung"* — the confound that broke the only sort before 0.900. It
was written into the brief and still fired on 5 of 11 spines and 7 of 11 draft Impulses. The pattern
in the Impulses was one mechanism: **every comparison or rationale requires a deciding self** —
*"than you need to"*, *"before anything else"*, *"because it can be"*, *"not to move in any way that
would…"*. A pull carries no argument for itself. Repaired below by stripping every than / before /
because / negative injunction. **Untested.**

**THE TRAP SCENARIO DID NOT FIRE.** The scenario built to reproduce the confound — she planned for
weeks to be at that place at that hour — produced the FEWEST agency flags of the three. The two
UNSOUGHT scenarios produced more. Whatever lets a character author their own state, it is not the
scenario's premise; it is in the block.

### Rung 9 (elation) — diagnosed, left in place

Depth 5 / 11 / 2 across three performed scenarios; unstable on every run. One judge named the
mechanism and it is about the axis, not the prose: *"Energy is not the same axis as depth… her
ongoing self and its projects are not eclipsed, they're supercharged. She is doing more of her own
agenda, not less."* On an axis of how much of you has been given over, elation **amplifies** the self
rather than surrendering it. `emotion-paths.md` flagged elation as *"the likeliest rung to fall"*.
Three runs agree. **Moving or removing a rung is the owner's decision; it is recorded here, not made.**

### Rung 6 (wonder) — diagnosed, spine repaired, untested

Read at depth 11 in one scenario with the character judged to have arranged it: *"surrender is
actively defended rather than suffered… a standing-apart self chooses, on purpose, not to explain."*
The designed spine — *you are not trying to make it fit* — **negates effort, and negation-of-effort
reads as chosen restraint.** The agency hold bites wonder through its own definition. Repaired to the
agentless *it has not been made to fit*, and the Belief from a decision (*I am not going to explain
it yet*) to a fact (*this does not have an explanation yet*). Untested.

## The spine: what has been given over, in order

```
permission → tilt → attention+valuation → senses → mood → frame → scale → whole present → tempo → steering → self
```

One faculty per rung, eleven rungs. Unlike DISPLEASURE there is no cost-then-gone two-step; each
rung is a different thing handed over. **Rungs 1–7 are thought; 8–11 are thought and action** — at 8
the body is stopped mid-motion, and from there the state has hands.

| # | rung | what is given over | band |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | receptivity | permission, and nothing beyond it | 0.00–0.08 |
| 2 | liking | your tilt | 0.08–0.17 |
| 3 | appreciation | your attention and your valuation | 0.17–0.26 |
| 4 | pleasure | your senses | 0.26–0.35 |
| 5 | gladness | your mood | 0.35–0.45 |
| 6 | wonder | your frame | 0.45–0.55 |
| 7 | awe | your scale | 0.55–0.64 |
| 8 | delight | your whole present, stopped mid-motion | 0.64–0.75 |
| 9 | elation | your tempo | 0.75–0.84 |
| 10 | rapture | your steering | 0.84–0.93 |
| 11 | ecstasy | your self | 0.93–1.00 |

Bands are the ones `emotion-paths.md` §3 assigned; copied, not re-derived. **Descent** —
contentment, bliss, savouring — is not on this ladder and is recorded there as a separate concern.

## Rules every block obeys

1. **Structure is ours; content is the actor's.** A block never names what the good thing is, where it
   came from, what is on offer, or who is present. *It* throughout is the actor's to fill.
2. **Nothing the character arranged.** Every state arrives or is found already in place. An Impulse is
   a pull, never a plan: no comparison, no rationale, no negative injunction.
3. **Four-case universal.** Every line holds for a bedridden elder, a ten-year-old, someone entirely
   alone, and a non-human source. No standing, walking, running, reaching, leaning.
4. **No object diction.** Not carry / set down / thing / get / have — the good thing may be a
   condition, a place, a sound, a future, or oneself.
5. **The name is never delivered.** `block_for` returns the text alone.

## The blocks

### 1 — receptivity

You are not closed, and you did not open. Something could get in; nothing has yet. Outwardly nothing shows at all. What would take this away is the smallest refusal.

- **The Sensation.** The face at rest. The breath unhurried and not held.
- **The Belief.** Nothing is wrong with letting this near. *It can come if it comes.*
- **The Impulse.** None yet.

### 2 — liking

Something has your leaning, and you did not decide to lean. Outwardly there is a slight turn toward it, easily missed. What would take this away is it turning out to be other than it seemed.

- **The Sensation.** A small ease in the chest that arrived without a reason.
- **The Belief.** This is on the better side of things. *I would not mind more of it.*
- **The Impulse.** To stay turned toward it.

### 3 — appreciation

It has your attention, and you are taking note of why. Outwardly you are pausing to look longer than an ordinary glance. What would take this away is finding it had nothing more behind it.

- **The Sensation.** The gaze steadies. A mild settling in the breath.
- **The Belief.** There is real quality here. *This is worth paying attention to.*
- **The Impulse.** To stay with it a moment longer.

### 4 — pleasure

Your body took it up before you had any view about it. Outwardly something in you has loosened — the shoulders, the mouth — without instruction. What would take this away is it stopping.

- **The Sensation.** Warmth where it touches. A softening you did not send for.
- **The Belief.** This is good. *That is the whole of it.*
- **The Impulse.** To let it go on.

### 5 — gladness

It has coloured the rest. Things unconnected to it come easier than they did an hour ago. Outwardly you are lighter with everything, not only with it. What would take this away is the colour draining as ordinary things take their weight back.

- **The Sensation.** A steady lift under the ribs that does not depend on where you look.
- **The Belief.** Things are all right — more than the one thing. *It is a good day.*
- **The Impulse.** To be easy with whatever comes next.

### 6 — wonder

It does not fit your ordinary expectations, and your mind pauses trying to match it. Outwardly you have stopped still, your attention held fast, with no word ready. What would take this away is reducing it to something familiar.

- **The Sensation.** A breath caught in suspension. An alert, open stillness through the chest.
- **The Belief.** This exceeds what I knew how to look for. *I do not have a measure for this.*
- **The Impulse.** To keep looking without trying to explain it.

### 7 — awe

You have gone small beside it, and being small is not the bad part. Outwardly you are very still and your gaze has gone long. What would take this away is it shrinking to your size.

- **The Sensation.** The chest opens as though to take in more air than it needs. A slight unsteadiness, as if held up by less than usual.
- **The Belief.** This is larger than me, and that is right. *I am allowed to be this small.*
- **The Impulse.** To be quiet.

### 8 — delight

It has stopped you where you are. What you were doing is not resumed. Outwardly your hands have stopped mid-task and you have not noticed. What would take this away is the interrupted thing reasserting itself.

- **The Sensation.** A brightness that arrives all at once, in the face and the hands. The breath catches upward.
- **The Belief.** Nothing else matters for this moment. *This is the whole of now.*
- **The Impulse.** Toward it, before any decision to move.

### 9 — elation

The thing has set your pace, and it is faster than everything around you. You cannot come down to meet it. Outwardly you are speaking and doing quicker than the room, and cannot slow when you try. What would take this away is the room catching up, or you running out.

- **The Sensation.** Heat in the face, breath high and quick, a hum through the limbs that wants somewhere to go.
- **The Belief.** Everything is possible right now. *There is no reason to hold back.*
- **The Impulse.** To do everything at once.

### 10 — rapture

It has taken over every channel of your attention; there is nothing left over to observe from or hold back with. Outwardly you are entirely swept up in it, unresponsive to whatever was happening around you. What would take this away is a shock violent enough to tear you out of it.

- **The Sensation.** A consuming fullness that fills the chest and ears like a tide, crowding out every other signal.
- **The Belief.** This has claimed the whole of me. *There is nothing else to hold onto.*
- **The Impulse.** To yield entirely to the sweep of it.

### 11 — ecstasy

The boundary between you and what is happening has completely dissolved under absolute saturation. There is no distance left to measure from, and no capacity to register anything outside this. Outwardly you are lost to the world, unreachable, as if the room had ceased to exist. What would take this away is only the inevitable exhaustion of the state itself.

- **The Sensation.** Total saturation. Every nerve carrying all it can bear, humming at full capacity.
- **The Belief.** All of me is consumed by this. *There is no boundary left.*
- **The Impulse.** None. Everything in you is already given over.

## The descent blocks

**The way down is not the climb reversed** (`docs/emotion-dynamics.md`; the rules are in
`DISPLEASURE.md` under the same heading). This is where the design's descent words — *contentment,
bliss, savouring* — live: they were never on the climb ladder, because you cannot savour what has not
already filled you. The pivot is **rung 9, elation**: the last rung at which there is still a you
setting a pace. Above it every channel has been given over and then the boundary itself, and neither
is handed back by the good thing stopping — they drain, and what is left is full. Above the pivot each
rung carries a second block for the mood that has stopped being fed; at and below elation, the climb
block serves both directions.

### 10 — rapture

**[State: rapture]**
The channels are being handed back one at a time. There is something left over to observe from now, and what it observes is the sweep, going out; you are still swept, and you know it. Outwardly you are coming back to whatever was happening around you, slowly, and not all the way. What ends this is the tide going all the way out, and it takes its own time.

- **The Sensation.** The fullness in the chest and ears receding. Other signals arriving at the edges and not yet in the middle.
- **The Belief.** This had the whole of me. *I can feel the edges of it.*
- **The Impulse.** To yield to what is left of it, and not to reach for anything else yet.

### 11 — ecstasy

**[State: ecstasy]**
The boundary is re-forming, and there is a you on this side of it again. What happened is still filling you and is no longer arriving; distance is possible, and you have not taken any. Outwardly you are reachable again and not yet reaching back, as if the room had only just resumed. What ends this is the fullness draining to where you can hold it — minutes to hours — and nothing hurries it.

- **The Sensation.** Saturation, draining. Every nerve still humming, below what it can bear now.
- **The Belief.** All of me was given to it. *I am still full of it.*
- **The Impulse.** None. To stay where it left you.
