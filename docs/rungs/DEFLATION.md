## What is measured, and what it cost to learn

**TWO SPINE ATTEMPTS FAILED; THE BLOCK ATTEMPT PERFORMS.** Attempt 1 (hurt-shaped rungs): 0 of 3
adversarial lenses. Attempt 2 (one-line spines on the wanting axis): text 0.976, reachability 0 of 6.

block/spine confound found on RECEPTIVITY holds on a third path: **author blocks, never spines.**

**THE SORT WAS INVALID ONCE, BY MY FRAME.** The first block sort returned rho −0.67 / −0.61 / −0.76.
I had framed the axis as *how much the person can still want* — a quantity that **falls** with rung —
and asked for floor→peak; every other path's axis rises. All three sorters put rungs 10 and 9 first.
Same blocks, frame restated in the design's words (*how much of you the lowness has taken*): 1.0 /
0.964 / 0.903. The number above is the valid one. The error is recorded because the next path with a
falling quantity will meet it.

**RUNG 6 HAD ONE DEFECT IN FOUR COSTUMES, AND THE FIFTH VERSION REMOVED IT.** *Inconsolability* is a
relational word — *cannot be consoled* needs a consoler. Every version of the block reached outside
the person for something to refuse: *not even its undoing* (the maximal remedy ruled out — read
terminal in 5 of 6 spine runs); *not on offer* (presupposes an offer — lens); *what is offered to you
does not land* (names an offer **and** an offerer — actors invented one in **all three** scenarios: a
dead spouse's dialogue for a man alone with failing eyes; neighbours at a burnt workshop; and the
invented offers not landing read as terminal). The fifth version is intra-personal — *you are not
moved by anything that is not it… the one thing — and it is not coming.* On the rerun: **no invented
person at rung 6 in either scenario, and rung 6 below rung 8 in both.** The diagnosis is confirmed by
the repair landing. Whether a consoler exists is the actor's.

**THE RESIDUAL IS THE HINGE, AND THE DESIGN NAMES IT.** In the `shop` scenario, and in the invalid
third scenario, rung 6 read as **more** able-to-want than rung 3 — reach 7 vs 5, future 6 vs 3. The
judges' reason: rung 6 is *"explicit, embodied want — the desiring apparatus fully online"*; rung 3 is
*"an approach-avoidance loop… it only repeated."* On the question they were asked — *can this person
still want* — **they are right.** Below the hinge the want does not weaken; it **concentrates**, from a
diffuse drop (1) to a single point (5), and rung 6 is the last live want. Capacity to want does not
fall until 7. So *can still want* measures the faculties half only, and applied to the scope half it
places the most concentrated want above the most diffuse. That is the design's hinge, measured.
**For the final pass:** the scope half wants its own question — *how much of their life is coloured
by it* — and the faculties half keeps *can still want / has a future*.

## Rules every block obeys

1. **Structure is ours; content is the actor's.** No block names what was lost, who caused it, what
   is on offer, or who is present. **Rung 6 in particular names no offer and no offerer** — that is
   the whole of its history.
2. **Not how much it hurts.** A loud middle and a quiet top are correct. Judges ranking by hurt will
   invert the ladder; the design warned of this and it was measured.
3. **Each rung has its own resolution, and it is a condition, not a scene.** Rung 5 resolves by
   having; rung 6 by the one thing that is not coming; rung 7 by being reached first; rung 8 by a
   later with you in it. No two share.
4. **Four-case universal, including a non-person cause and the outdoors.** Rung 9 no longer names a
   room.

## The blocks

### 1 — deflation

Something has gone out of you. The rest of you is working; whatever this is, it has not reached the rest yet. Outwardly nothing, or a pause. What would take this away is it coming back — and it may.

- **The Sensation.** A drop, small, where a lift had been. The breath lets out.
- **The Belief.** Something is less than it was. *Ah.*
- **The Impulse.** None. It is noted.

### 2 — dejection

You are not lifted by what would usually lift you. The things that work still happen; they arrive and do less. Outwardly you go through them. What would take this away is one of them landing.

- **The Sensation.** A heaviness in the limbs that was not there this morning. The face at rest, lower.
- **The Belief.** It should be working and it is not. *Not today.*
- **The Impulse.** To go through the motions.

### 3 — sadness

Something keeps pulling your attention back, and you get nothing from going there. Outwardly you are elsewhere, and then you are here again, and then elsewhere. What would take this away is it losing its pull.

- **The Sensation.** A returning ache, each time you go back. Between times, not much.
- **The Belief.** That is where it is. *There.*
- **The Impulse.** Back to it. And back.

### 4 — sorrow

It does not stop. It is there whether or not anything reminds you of it; the returning has become staying. Outwardly the pause has become the resting state. What would take this away is nothing that happens in a day — it will take longer than that.

- **The Sensation.** The ache has stopped arriving and started being. The chest holds it.
- **The Belief.** This is how it is now. *For now.*
- **The Impulse.** To carry it through the day.

### 5 — misery

Your wanting has closed down to a single point. Everything else has gone quiet; there is one thing you want, and it is the only thing. Outwardly you are hard to reach with anything that is not it. What would take this away is having it — and nothing else would.

- **The Sensation.** A pressure that has one direction. Everything else in the body is dim.
- **The Belief.** Only that. *Nothing else.*
- **The Impulse.** Toward it — the one thing.

### 6 — inconsolability

What you want is not going to come, and you know it. You want it anyway. Outwardly you are not moved by anything that is not it. What would take this away is the one thing — and it is not coming.

- **The Sensation.** The pressure with nowhere to go. The body braced against nothing arriving.
- **The Belief.** It is not coming. *And I still want it.*
- **The Impulse.** Toward what cannot come. Still.

### 7 — despondency

You have stopped reaching, though reaching is still possible. There are things within reach; you are not reaching. Outwardly you let things pass that you would once have taken. What would take this away is something reaching you first.

- **The Sensation.** Nothing pulls forward. The body is not against it; it is simply not toward it.
- **The Belief.** It would not help. *Why.*
- **The Impulse.** To let it pass.

### 8 — despair

Nothing ahead of you registers as yours. Whatever is coming is coming to someone; it does not read as coming to you. Outwardly you do not plan, do not prepare, do not look ahead. What would take this away is a later that has you in it.

- **The Sensation.** The body present, the days ahead not. A flatness with no edge to it.
- **The Belief.** There is no later. *Not mine.*
- **The Impulse.** None forward. Only what is already in the hands.

### 9 — devastation

Nothing stays in place long enough to be wanted. What arrives does not organise into anything you could reach for. Outwardly you are not tracking what is around you. What would take this away is the pieces settling — and they have not.

- **The Sensation.** The body reports things out of order. What is around you is not where it was.
- **The Belief.** Nothing holds long enough. *I cannot.*
- **The Impulse.** None that finishes. Starts, and stops.

### 10 — hollowness

There is no one in you to want. Not that the wanting is gone — the one who would want is not there. Outwardly you answer if spoken to, and there is no one behind the answer. What would take this away is someone coming back to be in there.

- **The Sensation.** There is no one to report from. The body goes on without you.
- **The Belief.** There is nothing to believe and no one to believe it. *—*
- **The Impulse.** None. There is no one to have one.

## The descent blocks

**The way down is not the climb reversed** (`docs/emotion-dynamics.md`; the rules are in
`DISPLEASURE.md` under the same heading). The pivot is **rung 8, despair**: the last rung with someone
in it to have a later. Above it the faculties half of the axis has taken the wanting itself and then
the one who wants, and those do not return by the lowness receding — they return late, and the return
is its own state. Above the pivot each rung carries a second block for the mood that has stopped being
fed; at and below despair, the climb block serves both directions.

### 9 — devastation

**[State: devastation]**
The pieces have started to hold, and what they hold is what happened. Nothing new arrives out of order; the old things are where the disorder left them, and they stay there now. Outwardly you are tracking what is around you again, and what you track is the wreck. What would take this away is the looking finishing, and it does not finish on your schedule.

- **The Sensation.** The body reports in order. The order is the wreck.
- **The Belief.** It holds now. *I can see it.*
- **The Impulse.** To look at it, and not touch.

### 10 — hollowness

**[State: hollowness]**
There is someone in you again, and they are not yet anyone. The answer, when you are spoken to, has something behind it now, and it is not you yet. Outwardly you are present in a way you were not, and no one could say who. What would take this away is the someone becoming you, which is not done by wanting it.

- **The Sensation.** The body notices itself being reported from. It is strange.
- **The Belief.** There is someone here. *Not yet me.*
- **The Impulse.** None the someone can name. To be where the body is.
